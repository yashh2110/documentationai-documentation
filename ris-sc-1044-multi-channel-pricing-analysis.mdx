## Overview

The **RIS-SC-1044 – Multi-Channel Pricing Analysis** script collects prices for SKUs across multiple channels, calculates min/max values, and highlights percentage differences.

<Callout kind="tip" collapsed="false">
  In simple terms:\
  See how each product is priced across Amazon, eBay, Etsy, and website channels.
</Callout>

## Purpose

This script:

- Retrieves product pricing from multiple sales channels

- Calculates price differences and variation percentages

- Highlights inconsistencies for analysis and corrective action

---

## When It Runs

Typically used for:

- Pricing audits

- Promotion planning

- Catalog or margin reviews

- Competitive pricing analysis

---

## Parameters

<ParamField path="SKU" param-type="string" required="false" deprecated="false">
  Product SKU(s) to analyze. If empty, the script processes all active SKUs.
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="ChannelList" param-type="string" required="false" deprecated="false">
  Comma-separated list of channels to include in the pricing comparison (e.g., Amazon,eBay,Etsy,Website).
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="IncludeVariations" param-type="boolean" required="false" deprecated="false">
  Whether to include eBay variation-level prices in the analysis. Default: True.
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="DateRange" param-type="string" required="false" deprecated="false">
  Optional date filter for price changes (e.g., last 30 days). Format: YYYY-MM-DD to YYYY-MM-DD.
</ParamField>

<Callout kind="info" collapsed="false">
  These parameters allow analysts to filter SKUs, select channels, include variations, and apply date ranges for flexible pricing comparisons.
</Callout>

---

## Simple Explanation

The script takes a product SKU, collects its price from multiple channels, compares all prices, and calculates the minimum, maximum, difference, and percentage variation.

---

## Step-by-Step Process

<Steps>
  <Step title="Filter Products" icon="filter" title-type="p">
    Use SKU parameter or select all active SKUs from StockItem.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Fetch Product Details" icon="info" title-type="p">
    Retrieve SKU, title, and barcode from StockItem.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Get Amazon & Etsy Prices" icon="shopping-cart" title-type="p">
    Use Stock\_ChannelList for channel-specific prices.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Get eBay Prices" icon="shopping-bag" title-type="p">
    Use Automation\_eBayListing\_Variaitions and Automation\_eBayListing for variation-level pricing.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Map Channel Data" icon="link" title-type="p">
    Use Stock\_ChannelSKU to link all channel SKUs to the internal SKU.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Combine Pricing Data" icon="merge" title-type="p">
    Merge all channel prices into a single dataset.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Calculate Minimum Price" icon="trending-down" title-type="p">
    Find the lowest price across channels.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Calculate Maximum Price" icon="trending-up" title-type="p">
    Find the highest price across channels.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Calculate Price Difference" icon="calculator" title-type="p">
    Compute difference between max and min price.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Calculate Percentage Difference" icon="percent" title-type="p">
    Calculate variation percentage.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Execute Query" icon="play" title-type="p">
    Generate final pricing comparison report.
  </Step>
</Steps>
