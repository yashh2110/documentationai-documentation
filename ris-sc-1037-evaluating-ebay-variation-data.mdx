## Overview

The **RIS-SC-1037 – Evaluating eBay Variation Data** script helps analysts understand eBay listing variations and their mapping to internal SKUs and TSINs.

<Callout kind="tip" collapsed="false">
  In simple terms:\
  See how eBay variation listings are structured and linked to inventory.
</Callout>

## Purpose

This script:

- Extracts variation attributes from eBay listings XML

- Maps variation SKUs to Linnworks SKUs and TSINs

- Provides structured reporting of variations for catalog analysis and troubleshooting

---

## When It Runs

Used for eBay audits, integration checks, and variation mapping validation.

**Typically runs:**

- Manually executed by analysts

- During eBay listing audits

- While troubleshooting variation mapping issues

- For catalog validation or migration projects

## Simple Explanation

The script retrieves eBay listings and their variations, extracts attributes like color and size from XML, maps variation SKUs to Linnworks SKUs and TSINs, and presents a structured overview for reporting or auditing purposes.

---

## Step-by-Step Process

<Steps>
  <Step title="Fetch Listings and Variations" icon="database" title-type="p">
    Join main listing and variation tables to get all eBay listings and associated variations.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Map to Linnworks SKU" icon="key" title-type="p">
    Use variation SKU mapping logic; fallback to barcode when variation SKU is missing.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Extract Variation Attributes" icon="edit" title-type="p">
    Parse XML to retrieve attributes such as color, size, and other variation details.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Get Category Info" icon="tag" title-type="p">
    Retrieve category details for each listing from category mapping table.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Rank Variations" icon="sort" title-type="p">
    Assign order to variations per listing for reporting purposes.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Map SKU and TSIN" icon="barcode" title-type="p">
    Use barcode as TSIN and map to internal SKU for accurate linkage.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Include Listing Metadata" icon="info" title-type="p">
    Add listing title, status, site, and account information to the report.
  </Step>
</Steps>
