## Overview

The **RIS-SC-1041 – Inventory Low Stock Report** script helps analysts quickly identify low stock items in the default warehouse and locate their bin positions for efficient restocking.

<Callout kind="tip" collapsed="false">
  In simple terms:\
  Track low inventory and know exactly where items are stored for fast replenishment.
</Callout>

## Purpose

This script:

- Detects SKUs with low stock  
- Excludes invalid or composite items  
- Shows bin/rack locations for efficient picking  

---

## When It Runs

Used for daily stock monitoring, warehouse audits, and replenishment planning.

**Typically runs:**

- Manually executed by analysts  
- During daily stock checks  
- Before purchase planning  
- During warehouse audits  
- For replenishment decisions  

---

## Parameters

<ParamField path="LowStockThreshold" param-type="int" required="true" deprecated="false">
  The numeric threshold to consider a SKU as low stock (e.g., 20 units).
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="WarehouseLocation" param-type="string" required="true" deprecated="false">
  The default warehouse location to filter stock levels.
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="BinFilter" param-type="string" required="false" deprecated="false">
  Optional filter for bin or rack codes (e.g., bins starting with "B").
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="IncludeCompositeItems" param-type="bool" required="false" deprecated="false">
  Flag to include or exclude composite/bundled SKUs. Default is false (exclude).
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="IncludeInactiveItems" param-type="bool" required="false" deprecated="false">
  Flag to include inactive or archived SKUs. Default is false (exclude).
</ParamField>

---

## Simple Explanation

The report checks all active products, filters those below the low stock threshold in the default warehouse, applies bin filters if provided, and outputs a list of SKUs with their bin locations for easy replenishment.

---

## Key Insight

<Callout kind="tip" collapsed="false">
By combining stock quantity thresholds with warehouse bin locations, this script enables fast decision-making for restocking while improving warehouse organization and efficiency.
</Callout>
