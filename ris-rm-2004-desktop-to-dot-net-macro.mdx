## Overview

The **RIS-RM-2004 – Desktop To Dot Net Macro** automatically assigns the appropriate packaging type and shipping service to orders.

It evaluates item quantity and product configuration to determine the correct packaging group, dimensions, weight, and shipping method.

<Callout kind="tip" collapsed="false">
  Ensure product extended properties (e.g., Shipping Type) and packaging mappings are correctly configured for accurate results.
</Callout>

## Purpose

This macro:

- Evaluates order item quantity

- Determines packaging type dynamically

- Assigns appropriate shipping service

👉 In short:\
Automatically decide packaging and shipping based on order contents.

## When It Runs

Used for packaging automation & shipping optimization

Typically runs:

- Manually triggered

- During order processing (before picking & shipping)

## Parameters

<ParamField path="OrderIds" param-type="string" required="true" deprecated="false">
  List of orders to be processed.
</ParamField>

<Callout kind="info" collapsed="false">
  No additional parameters are required.\
  The macro automatically determines packaging and shipping based on order data.
</Callout>

## How It Works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Calculate Item Quantity" icon="calculator" title-type="p">
    Determine total number of items in the order.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Handle Single Item Orders" icon="box" title-type="p">
    Apply predefined packaging and shipping service directly.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Evaluate Multi-Item Orders" icon="layers" title-type="p">
    Read product extended properties for all items.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Check Shipping Type" icon="search" title-type="p">
    Identify if items are marked as Automatic or Manual.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Apply Packaging Rules" icon="package" title-type="p">
    Apply packaging based on item count and shipping type rules.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Set Packaging Group" icon="box" title-type="p">
    Assign packaging group along with dimensions and weight.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Assign Shipping Service" icon="truck" title-type="p">
    Apply the appropriate shipping service based on selected packaging.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Packaging & Shipping Rules

<Callout kind="info" collapsed="false">
  Automatic Items:

  - 2–5 items → Small package + standard service

  - 6–8 items → Medium package + faster service

  - 9–26 items → Large package + priority service

  - 27+ items → Default/manual packaging

  Manual Items:

  - Default packaging with manual handling
</Callout>

## Final Result
