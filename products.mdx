## **Create and manage products**

Products can be created either from the backend or directly within the POS interface.

### **From the backend**

1. Go to **Point of Sale ‣ Products ‣ Products**

2. Click **New** to create a product, or open an existing one

3. Update the required fields

4. Ensure the **Point of Sale** option is enabled

### **From the POS interface**

1. Open the POS register

2. Click the menu icon (☰)

3. Select **Create Product**

4. Enter product details and click **Save**

The product becomes immediately available in the POS.

To edit a product:

- Long-click the product

- Select **Edit**

- Update details and save

## **POS product categories**

POS categories help organize products in the POS interface.

### **Create categories**

1. Go to **Point of Sale ‣ Configuration ‣ PoS Product Categories**

2. Click **New** or edit an existing category

3. Define hierarchy using the **Parent Category** field

Example:

- `Drinks` → `Hot Beverages`, `Soft Drinks`

### **Assign categories to products**

1. Open a product

2. Go to the **Point of Sale** tab

3. Select one or more categories in the **Category** field

### **Restrict visible categories**

To limit categories shown in the POS:

1. Go to <a href="https://www.odoo.com/documentation/19.0/applications/sales/point_of_sale/use.html#pos-use-settings" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">**POS Settings**</a>

2. In **Product & PoS Categories**, configure **Restrict Categories**

## **Product combos**

Product combos allow you to bundle multiple items into a single product.

Each combo includes:

- **Combo choices** (categories of items)

- Multiple selectable products within each choice

### **Example**

A meal combo may include:

- One burger

- One drink

- One portion of fries

Customers choose one item from each category.

### **Create combo choices**

1. Go to **Point of Sale ‣ Products ‣ Combo Choices**

2. Click **New**

3. Enter a name

4. Define:

   - **Maximum items** (max selections allowed)

   - **Included items** (default included quantity)

5. Add products under **Options**

6. Optionally set **Extra Price** per item

### **Pricing behavior**

- **Combo Price**:\
  Applied when customers select additional items beyond the included quantity\
  (based on the lowest-priced product in the choice)

- **Extra Price**:\
  Additional cost applied to specific items

### **Create a combo product**

1. Go to **Point of Sale ‣ Products ‣ Products**

2. Click **New**

3. Set **Product Type** to **Combo**

4. Select relevant **Combo Choices**

5. Define a **Sales Price**

6. Optionally assign a POS category

### **Use combos in POS**

1. Open the <a href="https://www.odoo.com/documentation/19.0/applications/sales/point_of_sale/use.html#pos-use-open-register" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">**POS register**</a>

2. Select the combo product

3. Choose items for each combo choice

4. Click **Add to Order**

**Note**

The final price depends on:

- Base **Sales Price**

- Additional selections (Combo Price)

- Items with **Extra Price**

### **Example**

An **Office Combo** priced at **300 €** includes desks and chairs.

- Chair combo allows up to 2 selections

- Cheapest chair defines the combo price (35 €)

- Premium chair has an extra price of 100 €

Selecting:

- Conference chair + armchair

Final price:

- 300 € + 35 € + 100 € = **435 €**

## **Serial numbers and lots**

Tracking with lots or serial numbers helps monitor product movement.

### **Enable tracking**

1. Enable <a href="https://www.odoo.com/documentation/19.0/applications/inventory_and_mrp/inventory/product_management/product_tracking/serial_numbers.html#inventory-product-management-traceability-setting" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">**Lots & Serial Numbers**</a>

2. Configure products and assign tracking numbers

### **Selling tracked products**

- When adding tracked products to POS, their lot/serial numbers are automatically loaded

- When loading a quotation or sales order, you are prompted to import tracking numbers

Once imported:

- Tracking numbers appear in the cart

- Displayed next to the product

### **Tracking status**

- 🟢 **Valid**: Tracking number is correctly assigned

- 🔴 **Invalid**: Missing or incorrect tracking

To modify:

- Click the tracking icon

- Select another lot or serial number

**Note**

Missing or incorrect tracking numbers do not block sales, but a warning must be confirmed before proceeding to payment.
