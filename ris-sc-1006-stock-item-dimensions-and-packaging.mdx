## Overview

The **RIS-SC-1006 – Stock Item Dimensions and Packaging** script provides a comprehensive report of product size, weight, packaging type, and storage location.

<Callout kind="tip" collapsed="false">
  In simple terms:\
  Get product dimensions, weight, packaging type, and bin location for active items.
</Callout>

## Purpose

This script:

- Retrieves SKU, depth, width, height, and weight

- Fetches packaging group information

- Retrieves bin or rack location in the warehouse

- Focuses only on active, non-composite items

👉 Supports warehouse packing, shipping calculations, and operational efficiency.

## When It Runs

Used for warehouse and operational reporting.

**Typically runs:**

- Manual execution for packing setup

- During shipping and courier calculation

- For warehouse organization and planning

## Parameters

<ParamField path="None" param-type="none" required="false" deprecated="false">
  No input parameters required.\
  Uses fixed filters:

  - Location = Default

  - Only active, non-composite items included
</ParamField>

<Callout kind="info" collapsed="false">
  The script consolidates product dimensions, packaging info, and storage location into a single report.
</Callout>

## Process Flow Step-by-Step

<Steps>
  <Step title="Get Product Details" icon="box" title-type="p">
    Retrieve SKU, depth, width, height, and weight for active items.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Get Packaging Info" icon="package" title-type="p">
    Fetch packaging group from PackageGroups table.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Get Bin Location" icon="map-pin" title-type="p">
    Retrieve bin or rack location from ItemLocation.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Apply Location Filter" icon="filter" title-type="p">
    Include only items from Default stock location.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Apply Item Filters" icon="slash" title-type="p">
    Exclude composite, deleted, and archived items.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Sort Results" icon="arrow-down" title-type="p">
    Order results by SKU in ascending order.
  </Step>
</Steps>
