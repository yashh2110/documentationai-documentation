## Overview

The **RIS-SC-2275 – Purchase Order Delivery Time Report** provides detailed insights into how long suppliers take to deliver goods.

It calculates the time between PO creation and stock receipt events, helping evaluate supplier performance and delivery efficiency.

<Callout kind="tip" collapsed="false">
  This report measures both <b>first delivery time</b> and <b>full completion time</b>, giving a complete view of supplier performance.
</Callout>

---

## Purpose

This report:

- Tracks PO delivery timelines  
- Measures supplier performance  
- Identifies delays in procurement  
- Provides item-level delivery completion insights  

👉 In short:  
See how fast suppliers deliver—from first item to full completion.

---

## When It Runs

Executed on-demand (SQL report)  

Typically used:

- Supplier performance analysis  
- Procurement monitoring  
- Delivery delay investigation  
- Lead time optimization  
- Brand-level supplier tracking

## Parameters

<ParamField path="FromDate" param-type="datetime" required="true">
  Start date for purchase orders.
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="ToDate" param-type="datetime" required="true">
  End date for purchase orders.
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="BrandName" param-type="string" required="false">

</ParamField>

## How It Works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Fetch Purchase Orders" icon="database" title-type="p">
    Retrieves PO details including supplier, value, and creation date.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Filter Data" icon="filter" title-type="p">
    Applies filters:
    - Date range  
    - Excludes cancelled POs  
    - Optional brand filter  
  </Step>

  <Step title="Calculate PO Metrics" icon="bar-chart" title-type="p">
    Computes:
    - Total items on PO  
    - Fully delivered items (Delivered ≥ Quantity)  
    - Total PO value  
  </Step>

  <Step title="Map PO Items" icon="list" title-type="p">
    Extracts distinct SKU-level items for each PO.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Track Stock Receipts" icon="download" title-type="p">
    Uses StockChange data to identify when stock was received.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Identify First & Last Receipt" icon="clock" title-type="p">
    Determines:
    - First item received date  
    - Final item received date  
  </Step>

  <Step title="Calculate Delivery Time" icon="timer" title-type="p">
    Computes:
    - Hours from PO open → first receipt  
    - Hours from PO open → full completion  
  </Step>

  <Step title="Prepare Final Output" icon="file-text" title-type="p">
    Combines supplier, PO, and timing data into final report.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Sort Results" icon="arrow-down" title-type="p">
    Orders by most recent purchase orders first.
  </Step>
</Steps>

---

## Important Notes

<Callout kind="warning" collapsed="false">
  - Only non-cancelled POs are included  

  - Delivered items are counted only when Delivered ≥ Quantity  

  - Receipt dates are derived from StockChange records  

  - Partial deliveries will show earlier "First Received" but later "Completed"  

  - If no stock receipt exists, dates may be NULL  

  - Time is calculated in hours using DATEDIFF  

  - Brand filter depends on extended property "brand"  
</Callout>

## Key Insight

<Callout kind="tip" collapsed="false">
  The most powerful aspect of this report is its ability to measure both partial and full delivery timelines. This dual insight helps distinguish between suppliers who deliver quickly but incompletely and those who complete orders efficiently—enabling smarter procurement decisions.
</Callout>
