## Overview

The **RIS-SC-1028 – Channel Order Details: Date Range** script helps analysts generate a comprehensive report of channel orders within a selected date range, including item, customer, shipping, and inventory information.

<Callout kind="tip" collapsed="false">
  In simple terms:\
  Get a complete view of orders and their details within any specified date range.
</Callout>

## Purpose

This script:

- Retrieves orders within a specified date range  
- Combines item-level, customer, shipping, and inventory data  
- Provides a detailed consolidated report  

---

## When It Runs

Used for sales reporting, finance, audits, and operational visibility.

**Typically runs:**

- Manually executed by analysts  
- During sales reporting  
- For finance and tax calculations  
- While auditing order details  
- For customer or shipment investigations  

---

## Parameters

<ParamField path="startDate" param-type="date" required="true" deprecated="false">
  Start date for filtering orders.
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="endDate" param-type="date" required="true" deprecated="false">
  End date for filtering orders.
</ParamField>

<Callout kind="info" collapsed="false">
  The script filters orders based on processed date and requires correct date format (YYYY-MM-DD).
</Callout>

---

## Simple Explanation

The script selects orders between the specified start and end dates, merges item, customer, shipping, and warehouse information, and creates a detailed report for analysis or reporting purposes.

---

## Step-by-Step Process

<Steps>
  <Step title="Set Date Range" icon="calendar" title-type="p">
    Filter orders between start date and end date.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Filter Order Status" icon="check-circle" title-type="p">
    Include only valid or processed orders.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Join Orders with Items" icon="layers" title-type="p">
    Combine order-level and item-level data.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Fetch Customer Details" icon="user" title-type="p">
    Retrieve name, address, email, and phone.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Get Shipping Information" icon="map-pin" title-type="p">
    Use carrier details or fallback to internal carrier data.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Resolve Channel Order ID" icon="link" title-type="p">
    Use available reference numbers for order identification.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Add Item-Level Details" icon="tag" title-type="p">
    Include SKU, quantity, price, title, and barcode.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Include Warehouse Information" icon="warehouse" title-type="p">
    Fetch bin or rack location for each item.
  </Step>
</Steps>

---

## Final Result

<Callout kind="success" collapsed="false">
The script generates a detailed report containing:

- Order ID  
- Channel order reference  
- Order date  
- SKU and quantity  
- Price and totals  
- Customer details  
- Shipping carrier  
- Warehouse location
</Callout>
