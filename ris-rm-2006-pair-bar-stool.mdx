## Overview

The **RIS-RM-2006 – Pair Bar Stool** macro converts individual order items into paired products using a predefined matching SKU.

It identifies eligible items, groups them into pairs, and restructures the order while maintaining accurate pricing and item details.

<Callout kind="tip" collapsed="false">
  Ensure the matching\_pair\_sku property is correctly configured for items to enable pairing.
</Callout>

## Purpose

This macro:

- Identifies items eligible for pairing

- Converts quantities into paired products

- Updates order structure accordingly

👉 In short:\
Automatically group items into pairs using matching SKU rules.

## When It Runs

Used for product bundling & order optimization

Typically runs:

- Manually triggered

- During order processing (before picking & packing)

## Parameters

<ParamField path="OrderIds" param-type="string" required="true" deprecated="false">
  List of orders to be processed.
</ParamField>

<Callout kind="info" collapsed="false">
  No external input is required.\
  The macro operates based on item configuration within the system.
</Callout>

## How It Works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Process Items" icon="download" title-type="p">
    Iterate through all items in each order.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Check Pair Configuration" icon="search" title-type="p">
    Look for matching\_pair\_sku in item extended properties.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Identify Pair SKU" icon="link" title-type="p">
    Retrieve corresponding paired SKU from the system.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Validate Quantity" icon="check-circle" title-type="p">
    ✔ Quantity ≥ 2 → proceed with pairing\
    ❌ Otherwise → skip pairing
  </Step>

  <Step title="Create Pairs" icon="layers" title-type="p">
    Convert items into pairs based on quantity.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Calculate Pricing" icon="calculator" title-type="p">
    Set pair price = 2 × single item price while retaining discounts and taxes.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Update Order" icon="save" title-type="p">
    Add paired items, retain leftover single items, and remove original items.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Pairing Logic

<Callout kind="info" collapsed="false">
  Example:\
  Quantity = 5

  👉 Result:

  - 2 paired items

  - 1 remaining single item

  Pair price = 2 × individual item price
</Callout>

## Usefulness

This macro is highly beneficial for:

- Supporting bundle and paired product sales

- Simplifying order structure

- Improving warehouse picking accuracy

- Reducing manual adjustments

- Ensuring consistent SKU usage

## Important Notes

<Callout kind="warning" collapsed="false">
  - Works only if matching\_pair\_sku is configured

  - Applies only when quantity ≥ 2

  - Odd quantities leave one item unpaired

  - Original items are removed after conversion
</Callout>
