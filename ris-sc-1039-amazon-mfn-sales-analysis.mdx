## Overview

The **RIS-SC-1039 – Amazon MFN Sales Analysis** script helps analysts evaluate Amazon FBM (Merchant Fulfilled Network) performance, while also checking for FBA and Prime-enabled listings.

<Callout kind="tip" collapsed="false">
  In simple terms:\
  Understand MFN sales performance, compare FBM with FBA/Prime, and analyze pricing, sales volume, and stock levels.
</Callout>

---

## Purpose

This script:

- Filters Amazon orders for the last 3 months

- Excludes FBA warehouse orders

- Maps SKUs to ASINs

- Identifies FBA and Prime flags for listings

- Retrieves price and stock data

- Aggregates units sold, orders, and revenue

---

## When It Runs

Used for sales performance analysis, inventory planning, and marketplace strategy reviews.

**Typically runs:**

- Manually executed by analysts

- During monthly Amazon performance reviews

- While comparing FBM vs FBA strategy

- For pricing, inventory, and fulfillment planning

## Parameters

<ParamField path="@Source" param-type="string" required="true" deprecated="false">
  Marketplace source to filter orders .
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="@Subsource" param-type="string" required="true" deprecated="false">
  Amazon channel or subsource .
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="@FBALocationName" param-type="string" required="true" deprecated="false">
  Warehouse location to exclude FBA orders .
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="@PrimeTemplate" param-type="string" required="true" deprecated="false">
  Shipping template name used to flag Prime listings .
</ParamField>

<Callout kind="info" collapsed="false">
  Analysts can modify these parameters to run the report for different Amazon subsources, FBA locations, or Prime templates.
</Callout>

---

## Simple Explanation

The script pulls Amazon MFN orders from the last 3 months, filters out FBA orders, maps SKUs to ASINs, flags listings as FBA or Prime, adds price and stock information, and produces a detailed sales performance report.

---

## Step-by-Step Process

<Steps>
  <Step title="Set Filters" icon="filter" title-type="p">
    Use the Order table to filter by Source (@Source) and Subsource (@Subsource).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Apply Date Filter" icon="calendar" title-type="p">
    Include only orders processed in the last 3 months.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Filter MFN Orders" icon="x-circle" title-type="p">
    Exclude FBA warehouse orders using @FBALocationName.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Map SKU to ASIN" icon="link" title-type="p">
    Use Stock\_ChannelSKU to map internal SKUs to Amazon ASINs.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Identify FBA Listings" icon="star" title-type="p">
    Use item\_additional to detect FBA flag for each ASIN.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Identify Prime Listings" icon="star" title-type="p">
    Use download.item with ShippingGroupName matching @PrimeTemplate to flag Prime listings.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Fetch Price Data" icon="dollar-sign" title-type="p">
    Retrieve Amazon price from item\_price table.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Fetch Stock Data" icon="box" title-type="p">
    Retrieve stock levels from StockLevel for the default warehouse.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Aggregate Sales Data" icon="bar-chart" title-type="p">
    Calculate total units sold, number of orders, and revenue for each SKU/ASIN.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Execute Query" icon="play" title-type="p">
    Combine all data to generate the final MFN sales performance report.
  </Step>
</Steps>
