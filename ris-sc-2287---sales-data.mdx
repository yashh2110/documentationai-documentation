## Overview

The **SKU Sales Quantity Report** provides a consolidated view of product performance by calculating total quantities sold across both open and processed orders.

It focuses only on active, non-composite SKUs and supports supplier-based filtering.

<Callout kind="tip" collapsed="false">
  Total Quantity Sold = Open Order Qty + Processed Order Qty
</Callout>

---

## Purpose

This report:

- Combines open and processed order data  
- Calculates total quantity sold per SKU  
- Filters SKUs by supplier (optional)  
- Excludes inactive, archived, and composite items  

👉 In short:  
Get total SKU sales volume across all order stages.

---

## When It Runs

Executed on-demand (SQL report)

Typically used:

- Sales performance analysis  
- Inventory planning  
- Supplier-based reporting  
- Demand forecasting  
- Stock replenishment decisions

## How It Works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Prepare Date Range" icon="calendar" title-type="p">
    Adjusts input dates to cover full days:
    - Start → beginning of day  
    - End → end of day (23:59:59)
  </Step>

  <Step title="Filter Master SKUs" icon="package" title-type="p">
    Selects only valid SKUs:
    - Not deleted  
    - Not archived  
    - Not composite (bundle components excluded)  
  </Step>

  <Step title="Apply Supplier Filter" icon="filter" title-type="p">
    Filters SKUs based on supplier mapping:
    - Includes all if "All" selected  
    - Otherwise matches supplier name  
  </Step>

  <Step title="Calculate Open Order Quantity" icon="clock" title-type="p">
    Retrieves quantities from open orders within date range.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Calculate Processed Order Quantity" icon="check-circle" title-type="p">
    Retrieves quantities from processed orders:
    - Excludes cancelled orders (status ≠ 0)
  </Step>

  <Step title="Combine Quantities" icon="plus-circle" title-type="p">
    Total Qty Sold = Open Qty + Processed Qty
  </Step>

  <Step title="Filter Final Results" icon="list" title-type="p">
    Includes only SKUs where total quantity > 0.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Sort Output" icon="arrow-down" title-type="p">
    Orders results by highest selling SKUs first.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Important Notes

<Callout kind="warning" collapsed="false">
  - Includes both open and processed orders  

  - Cancelled orders are excluded (status = 0)  

  - Composite/bundle SKUs are excluded  

  - Supplier filter depends on ItemSupplier mapping  

  - SKUs with zero sales are excluded  

  - Purchase price is shown for reference only (not used in calculation)  
</Callout>
