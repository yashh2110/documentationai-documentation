## Overview

The **RIS-SC-1043 – Amazon SKU Mapping** script helps analysts and operations teams validate that Amazon SKUs are properly linked to internal Linnworks SKUs.

<Callout kind="tip" collapsed="false">
  In simple terms:\
  See how Amazon SKUs (custom labels) are connected to internal Linnworks SKUs.
</Callout>

## Purpose

This script:

- Retrieves Amazon custom label SKUs

- Maps them to internal Linnworks SKUs

- Validates SKU mapping accuracy for the Amazon Subsource account

---

## When It Runs

Used for integration checks, catalog audits, and bulk listing validations.

**Typically runs:**

- Manually executed by analysts

- During Amazon integration checks

- While fixing SKU mapping issues

- Before bulk listing updates

- During catalog audits

---

## Parameters

<ParamField path="Source" param-type="string" required="true" deprecated="false">
  Channel source name. Example: 'AMAZON'.
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="Subsource" param-type="string" required="true" deprecated="false">
  Channel subsource or account name.
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="IncludeUnmapped" param-type="bool" required="false" deprecated="false">
  Flag to include SKUs that are not yet mapped to internal SKUs. Default is true.
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="SortBySKU" param-type="bool" required="false" deprecated="false">
  Flag to sort the report by internal SKU (ItemNumber). Default is true.
</ParamField>

---

## Simple Explanation

The script retrieves all Amazon SKUs from the Subsource account, checks for mapping to internal Linnworks SKUs, and generates a report showing mapped and unmapped items.

---

## Step-by-Step Process

<Steps>
  <Step title="Fetch Channel Listings" icon="download" title-type="p">
    Use Stock\_ChannelList to get Amazon SKUs (custom labels).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Match Channel SKU Mapping" icon="link" title-type="p">
    Join Stock\_ChannelList with Stock\_ChannelSKU using ChannelSKU.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Link to Internal SKU" icon="database" title-type="p">
    Use StockItem to retrieve ItemNumber (Linnworks SKU).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Apply Channel Filter" icon="filter" title-type="p">
    Filter records for Amazon Subsource account.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Validate Mapping" icon="check-circle" title-type="p">
    Identify mapped and unmapped SKUs.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Sort Output" icon="sort-amount-up" title-type="p">
    Order results by SKU for clarity.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Execute Query" icon="play" title-type="p">
    Generate final mapping report.
  </Step>
</Steps>
