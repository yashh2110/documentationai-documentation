## Overview

The **RIS-SC-1036 – Count of Paid Orders by Fulfillment Location** script helps analysts understand the distribution of paid orders across different fulfillment types and shipping methods.

<Callout kind="tip" collapsed="false">
  In simple terms:\
  See how many paid orders fall into each fulfillment category.
</Callout>

## Purpose

This script:

- Counts paid orders  
- Categorizes orders by fulfillment type (TS Prime, WD Prime, Premium, Standard)  
- Summarizes totals for each category  
- Helps monitor fulfillment distribution  

---

## When It Runs

Used for daily order analysis, performance tracking, and reporting.

**Typically runs:**

- Manually executed by analysts  
- During daily order reviews  
- While tracking fulfillment performance  
- For shipping strategy and management reporting  

---

## Parameters

<ParamField path="None" param-type="none" required="false" deprecated="false">
The script uses predefined categorization logic and does not require any manual input.
</ParamField>

<Callout kind="info" collapsed="false">
  Fixed rules are applied for order categorization based on fulfillment type and shipping method.
</Callout>

---

## Simple Explanation

The script collects all paid orders, identifies their fulfillment type (TS Prime, WD Prime, Premium, Standard), and counts the number of orders in each category to provide operational insight.

---

## Step-by-Step Process

<Steps>
  <Step title="Filter Paid Orders" icon="filter" title-type="p">
    Select orders with paid status.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Collect Base Data" icon="database" title-type="p">
    Prepare the dataset of all paid orders.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Identify TS Prime Orders" icon="star" title-type="p">
    Filter Prime orders originating from the Trimming Shop.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Identify WD Prime Orders" icon="star" title-type="p">
    Filter Prime orders from other channels (non-Trimming Shop).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Identify Premium Orders" icon="premium" title-type="p">
    Select non-Prime orders using premium carriers.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Identify Standard Orders" icon="package" title-type="p">
    Select remaining non-Prime orders.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Filter by Location" icon="map" title-type="p">
    Include only orders from the default warehouse location.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Calculate Counts" icon="calculator" title-type="p">
    Aggregate total orders per fulfillment category.
  </Step>
</Steps>
