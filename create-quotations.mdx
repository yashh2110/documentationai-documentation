In *Odoo Sales*, quotations are used to present pricing and product details to customers. Once a quotation is approved, it is automatically converted into a *sales order*, which can then be invoiced and processed for payment.

## **Quotation settings**

To configure quotation-related options, go to:\
**Sales app ‣ Configuration ‣ Settings**, then scroll to the **Quotations & Orders** section.

<Image src="https://www.odoo.com/documentation/19.0/_images/quotations-orders-section.png" width="745" height="590" alt="The Quotations and Orders section on the Odoo Sales app Settings page." />

Available options include:

- **Quotation Templates**: Create reusable templates with predefined products and terms. Enabling this adds a **Default Template** option and access to template management.

- **Online Signature**: Allow customers to confirm quotations with a digital signature.

- **Online Payment**: Enable customers to make advance payments (full or partial). A **Prepayment (%)** field becomes available when activated.

- **Default Quotation Validity**: Define how long quotations remain valid (in days).

- **Default Recurrence**: Set a default billing cycle for recurring products.

- **Sale Warnings**: Display alerts when certain customers or products are used.

- **PDF Quote Builder**: Customize the design of quotation documents.

- **Lock Confirmed Sales**: Prevent edits after a quotation is confirmed.

- **Pro-Forma Invoice**: Allow sending pro-forma invoices to customers.

Enable the required options and click **Save** to apply changes.

## **Quotations dashboard**

When opening the **Sales app**, the *Quotations* dashboard is displayed by default.

It initially shows records filtered by **My Quotations**, meaning only quotations assigned to the current user are visible.

<Image src="https://www.odoo.com/documentation/19.0/_images/quotations-dashboard.png" width="928" height="362" alt="The Quotations dashboard present in the Odoo Sales application." />

**Note**

To view all quotations, remove the **My Quotations** filter from the search bar.

You can switch between multiple views such as:

- List

- Kanban

- Calendar

- Pivot

- Graph

- Activity

Click any quotation to open and edit its details.

## **Create quotation**

To create a new quotation:

1. Open the **Sales app**

2. Click **New** (available in list or Kanban view)

This opens a blank quotation form.

<Image src="https://www.odoo.com/documentation/19.0/_images/quotation-form.png" width="786" height="589" alt="A typical quotation form in the Odoo Sales application." />

Start by filling in the required details:

- **Customer** (mandatory)

- **Invoice Address** and **Delivery Address** (auto filled if customer exists)

If applicable:

- Add a **Referrer**, which enables a **Commission Plan** field

- Select a **Quotation Template**

- Choose a **Recurring Plan** (for subscriptions)

- Apply a **Pricelist**

- Set **Payment Terms**

The **Expiration Date** is automatically calculated based on system settings or the selected template.

### **Order Lines tab**

Use this tab to add products to the quotation.

You can:

- Click **Add a product** and select items manually

- Use **Catalog** to browse products visually

<Image src="https://www.odoo.com/documentation/19.0/_images/product-catalog.png" width="928" height="324" alt="A product catalog accessible via a quotation in the Odoo Sales application." />

Additional options:

- **Add a section** to group products

- **Add a note** for specific product lines

- Apply **Discounts**, **Coupons**, **Promotions**, or **Shipping**

### **Optional Products tab**

This tab allows you to suggest additional products that complement the main purchase, helping increase order value.

Example: Offering accessories alongside a primary product.

### **Other Info tab**

This tab contains advanced configuration options grouped into four sections.

#### **Sales section**

<Image src="https://www.odoo.com/documentation/19.0/_images/other-info-sales.png" width="480" height="403" alt="The Sales section of the Other Info tab of a quotation form in Odoo Sales." />

- **Salesperson**: Assigned automatically but can be changed

- **Sales Team**: Linked to the selected salesperson

- **Company**: Visible in multi-company environments

- **Online Signature / Payment**: Available if enabled in settings

- **Customer Reference**: Custom identifier

- **Tags**: Useful for organization and filtering

#### **Delivery section**

<Image src="https://www.odoo.com/documentation/19.0/_images/other-info-delivery.png" width="663" height="305" alt="The Delivery section of the Other Info tab of a quotation form in Odoo Sales." />

- **Shipping Weight**: Calculated automatically

- **Incoterm** and **Location**: Used for international trade

- **Shipping Policy**:

  - *As soon as possible*

  - *When all products are ready*

- **Delivery Date**: Manually selectable if needed

#### **Invoicing section**

<Image src="https://www.odoo.com/documentation/19.0/_images/other-info-invoicing.png" width="675" height="175" alt="The Invoicing section of the Other Info tab of a quotation form in Odoo Sales." />

- **Fiscal Position**: Adjust taxes based on customer location

- **Analytic Account**: Track financial data for reporting

#### **Tracking section**

<Image src="https://www.odoo.com/documentation/19.0/_images/other-info-tracking.png" width="486" height="286" alt="The Tracking section of the Other Info tab of a quotation form in Odoo Sales." />

- **Source Document**: Reference origin

- **Opportunity**: Link to CRM record

- **Campaign / Medium / Source**: Marketing tracking fields

### **Notes tab**

Use this section to add internal comments or important details related to the quotation or customer.

## **Sending and confirming quotations**

Once the quotation is ready, use the action buttons at the top:

- **Send by Email**: Opens a pre-filled email with the quotation attached as a PDF

- **Send PRO-FORMA Invoice**: Available if enabled; sends a pro-forma version

- **Confirm**: Converts the quotation into a **Sales Order**

- **Preview**: Displays the customer-facing version

- **Cancel**: Cancels the quotation

**Note**

If **Lock Confirmed Sales** is enabled, confirmed orders cannot be edited.

After confirmation, the quotation becomes a sales order and is ready for invoicing and payment processing.
