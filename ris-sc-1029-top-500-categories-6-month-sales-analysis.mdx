## Overview

The **RIS-SC-1029 – Top 500 Categories: 6-Month Sales Analysis** script helps analysts identify top-selling products and categories, evaluate sales trends, and align inventory with demand.

<Callout kind="tip" collapsed="false">
  In simple terms:\
  Determine best-selling products and categories over the last 6 months to support planning and forecasting.
</Callout>

## Purpose

This script:

- Analyzes sales data from the last 6 months  
- Groups SKUs by category  
- Ranks products based on sales performance  
- Provides current stock and suggested minimum stock level insights  

---

## When It Runs

Used for business analysis, inventory planning, and category performance reviews.

**Typically runs:**

- Manually executed by analysts  
- During monthly or quarterly sales reviews  
- For inventory planning and forecasting  
- While identifying best-selling products  

---

## Parameters

<ParamField path="None" param-type="fixed" required="false" deprecated="false">
  Uses automatic 6-month date logic; no manual input required.
</ParamField>

<Callout kind="info" collapsed="false">
  The script dynamically calculates the last 6 months from the current date for analysis.
</Callout>

---

## Simple Explanation

The script checks sales for the last 6 months, groups products by category, ranks SKUs based on units sold, and compares sales with current stock levels to provide actionable insights for inventory management.

---

## Step-by-Step Process

<Steps>
  <Step title="Filter Last 6 Months Orders" icon="calendar" title-type="p">
    Include only orders processed within the last 6 months.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Link SKUs with Orders" icon="link" title-type="p">
    Combine product, order, and quantity data for analysis.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Get Product Category" icon="tag" title-type="p">
    Group SKUs based on their category from extended properties.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Exclude Unwanted Data" icon="alert-circle" title-type="p">
    Remove composite child items and exclude internal fulfillment data.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Fetch Current Stock Level" icon="warehouse" title-type="p">
    Retrieve stock quantities from default warehouse location.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Calculate Metrics" icon="calculator" title-type="p">
    - Total units sold  
    - Number of orders  
    - Total revenue  
    - Suggested minimum stock level
  </Step>

  <Step title="Rank SKUs Within Category" icon="award" title-type="p">
    Rank products based on units sold within each category.
  </Step>
</Steps>
