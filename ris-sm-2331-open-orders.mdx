## Overview

The **RIS-SM-2331 – Open Orders&#x20;**&#x20;macro monitors all open orders across warehouses and flags those that remain unprocessed beyond a configured time threshold.

It automatically filters excluded channels, generates a CSV report, sends email alerts, and tags processed orders to prevent duplicate notifications.

<Callout kind="tip" collapsed="false">
  Configure excluded sales channels and threshold hours carefully to ensure alerts are accurate and actionable.
</Callout>

## Purpose

This macro:

- Monitors open orders across all warehouses

- Detects delayed orders beyond the defined time threshold

- Excludes orders from specified sales channels

- Sends automated email alerts with CSV attachment

- Marks processed orders to avoid duplicate alerts

👉 In short:\
Detect delayed orders → generate report → send alert → mark orders.

## When It Runs

Typically scheduled:

- Hourly or daily

- For monitoring open orders across warehouses

- To detect delayed fulfilment

- For proactive reporting and alerting

## How It Works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Input Validation" icon="check-circle" title-type="p">
    Validate threshold hours, excluded sources, and email configuration. Stop if invalid.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Fetch Locations" icon="map" title-type="p">
    Retrieve all warehouse locations to prepare for order processing.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Apply Filters" icon="filter" title-type="p">
    Select orders older than defined hours and exclude already processed orders. Apply date and status filters.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Fetch Open Orders" icon="list" title-type="p">
    Loop through each location, retrieve open orders, and combine results.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Order Filtering" icon="search" title-type="p">
    Remove excluded sources (e.g., Amazon), calculate order age, and keep only delayed orders.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Identifier Check" icon="tag" title-type="p">
    Ensure identifier exists to prevent duplicate processing.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Email Preparation" icon="mail" title-type="p">
    Generate CSV file with order data, create HTML email body, and include summary table.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Email Validation" icon="alert-circle" title-type="p">
    Remove duplicate email addresses, validate format, and stop if invalid.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Send Email" icon="send" title-type="p">
    Send alert email with order summary and CSV attachment. Log success or failure.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Mark Orders" icon="check" title-type="p">
    Assign identifier to processed orders to prevent duplicate alerts.
  </Step>
</Steps>
