## Overview

The **RIS-RM-1004 – Set Cheapest Shipping Price** macro automatically determines and applies the most suitable shipping method for each order.

It evaluates available shipping options in real-time and selects the best option based on cost efficiency or predefined business rules.

<Callout kind="tip" collapsed="false">
  Ensure shipping integrations are properly configured to retrieve accurate real-time quotes.
</Callout>

## Purpose

This macro:

- Retrieves available shipping options

- Evaluates them based on cost or business rules

- Applies the most suitable shipping method

👉 In short:\
Automatically choose the best (usually cheapest) shipping option.

## When It Runs

Used for shipping optimization & automation

Typically runs:

- Manually triggered

- During order processing (before label generation)

## Parameters

<ParamField path="OrderIds" param-type="string" required="true" deprecated="false">
  List of orders to be processed.
</ParamField>

<Callout kind="info" collapsed="false">
  No additional parameters are required.\
  The macro automatically determines the optimal shipping option.
</Callout>

## How It Works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Evaluate Orders" icon="search" title-type="p">
    Retrieve order details such as weight, destination, and current shipping method.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Retrieve Integrations" icon="settings" title-type="p">
    Identify all configured shipping providers.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Get Shipping Quotes" icon="activity" title-type="p">
    Request real-time shipping rates for each order.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Apply Selection Logic" icon="filter" title-type="p">
    ✔ If business rules exist → prioritise preferred services\
    ✔ Otherwise → select the most cost-effective option
  </Step>

  <Step title="Apply Shipping Method" icon="truck" title-type="p">
    Update order with selected shipping service and cost.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Track Changes" icon="database" title-type="p">
    Store previous and updated shipping details for auditing.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Handle Exceptions" icon="alert-circle" title-type="p">
    If no suitable option is found → flag order for manual review.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Final Result

<Callout kind="success" collapsed="false">
  - Orders assigned optimal shipping method

  - Shipping costs minimized

  - Orders ready for label generation

  - Exceptions clearly identified
</Callout>

## Usefulness

This macro is highly beneficial for:

- Reducing shipping costs

- Eliminating manual selection effort

- Speeding up order processing

- Ensuring consistent shipping decisions

- Improving visibility of problematic orders

## Important Notes

<Callout kind="warning" collapsed="false">
  - Requires properly configured shipping integrations

  - Depends on accurate order data (weight, address, etc.)

  - Orders are flagged if no quotes are returned

  - Supports custom business priority rules

  - Does not modify or split order items
</Callout>
