## Overview

The **RIS-SC-2276 – Net Revenue Report** provides a daily financial summary of sales performance by calculating net revenue after accounting for discounts and refunds.

It aggregates order-level and item-level data to give a clear picture of actual earnings.

<Callout kind="tip" collapsed="false">
  Net Revenue = Sales Revenue - Discounts - Refunds  
  (Tax is shown separately for clarity)
</Callout>

---

## Purpose

This report:

- Calculates total sales revenue from order items  
- Subtracts discounts and refunds to compute net revenue  
- Provides daily aggregated financial insights  
- Supports optional filtering by brand  

👉 In short:  
Track real earnings per day after deductions.

## How It Works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Initialize Date Range" icon="calendar" title-type="p">
    Sets default date range if parameters are not provided.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Fetch Base Order Data" icon="database" title-type="p">
    Retrieves processed orders and calculates line-level revenue:
    Qty × Price per Unit.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Apply Brand Filter" icon="filter" title-type="p">
    Filters data based on brand using extended properties.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Aggregate Revenue" icon="bar-chart" title-type="p">
    Groups data by sale date and calculates:
    - Total Revenue  
    - Total Tax  
    - Total Discounts  
  </Step>

  <Step title="Fetch Refund Data" icon="rotate-ccw" title-type="p">
    Retrieves refund transactions and aggregates them per day.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Calculate Net Revenue" icon="calculator" title-type="p">
    Net Revenue = Revenue - Discount - Refund
  </Step>

  <Step title="Prepare Final Output" icon="file-text" title-type="p">
    Formats values and returns daily financial metrics.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Important Notes

<Callout kind="warning" collapsed="false">
  - Revenue is calculated at item level (Qty × Price)  

  - Discounts are taken from order-level fields  

  - Refunds are grouped by refund date (not order date)  

  - Tax is not deducted in net revenue (reported separately)  

  - Brand filter depends on extended property accuracy  

  - Includes only processed orders (status 1, 2, 4)  
</Callout>

## Example Scenario

<Expandable title="Daily Revenue Calculation Example" default-open="false">
  Date: 2025-09-10  

  Orders:
  - Sales Revenue: £1000  
  - Discounts: £100  
  - Refunds: £50  

  👉 Calculation:
  Net Revenue = 1000 - 100 - 50 = £850  

  Result:
  - Total Sales Revenue → £1000  
  - Total Discounts → £100  
  - Total Refunds → £50  
  - Net Revenue → £850  

  Insight:
  Provides a clear view of actual earnings after deductions.
</Expandable>
