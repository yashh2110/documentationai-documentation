## Overview

The **RIS-RM-2032 – GenericDropshipEmail** macro automates supplier communication in dropshipping workflows by generating and sending purchase order emails.

It prepares a structured email containing order, item, and delivery details, and sends it to the appropriate supplier based on predefined logic.

<Callout kind="tip" collapsed="false">
  Ensure supplier email configuration, SKU mapping, and category logic are correctly set before running this macro.
</Callout>

---

## Purpose

This macro:

- Generates supplier-specific dropship emails  
- Includes item and delivery details in email body  
- Sends emails to appropriate suppliers  
- Assigns processed orders to a folder  

👉 In short:  
Automatically send supplier emails and organize orders.

---

## When It Runs

Used in dropshipping workflows  

Typically runs:

- Manually triggered  
- Before dispatch  
- During supplier communication process  

---

## Parameters

<ParamField path="OrderIds" param-type="string" required="true" deprecated="false">
  List of orders to process.
</ParamField>

<Callout kind="info" collapsed="false">
  Folder name is predefined as <b>"Jills Sinks & Taps"</b>.
</Callout>

## How It Works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Initialize Email Settings" icon="settings" title-type="p">
    Define supplier email configuration and setup.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Process Orders" icon="list" title-type="p">
    Loop through each OrderId and fetch order details.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Prepare Email Subject" icon="edit" title-type="p">
    Create email subject using OrderNumber.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Build Email Body" icon="file-text" title-type="p">
    Construct email content including items and order details.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Analyze Items" icon="search" title-type="p">
    Evaluate SKUs and categories to determine supplier.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Select Supplier" icon="user-check" title-type="p">
    Use predefined logic to choose correct supplier email.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Add Delivery Details" icon="map-pin" title-type="p">
    Include full customer address and delivery information.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Assign Folder" icon="folder" title-type="p">
    Assign order to <b>"Jills Sinks & Taps"</b> folder.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Send Email" icon="send" title-type="p">
    Send formatted email to supplier.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Log Execution" icon="file" title-type="p">
    Log execution details, success, and errors.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Important Notes

<Callout kind="warning" collapsed="false">
  - Processes all orders due to forced flag  

  - Uses hardcoded SKU and category logic  

  - Requires correct category matching  

  - Email configuration is hardcoded  

  - No duplicate email prevention  

  - No retry mechanism for failed emails  

  - Folder name is fixed  

  - Logic depends on SKU mapping  
</Callout>
