## **Supported features**

The **Shopee Connector** provides the following capabilities:

- Automatically imports confirmed Shopee orders into Odoo, including:

  - Product name

  - SKU reference

  - Quantity ordered

- Synchronizes product stock levels from Odoo to Shopee for merchant-fulfilled operations (<u>FBM</u>)

- Allows connection of multiple Shopee seller accounts

- Supports multiple shops (marketplaces) under a single seller account

**Note**

The **Shopee Connector** focuses on synchronizing sales order data. Other operations—such as generating financial reports, managing disputes, or processing refunds—must still be handled through *Shopee Seller Central*.
