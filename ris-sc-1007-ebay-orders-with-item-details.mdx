## Overview

The **RIS-SC-1007 – eBay Orders with Item Details** script provides a comprehensive view of eBay orders, combining order, item, customer, shipping, and XML data.

<Callout kind="tip" collapsed="false">
  In simple terms:\
  Get full eBay order details including items, customer, shipping, and additional eBay-specific information.
</Callout>

## Purpose

This script:

- Retrieves order ID, channel order ID, order dates, totals, tax, and currency

- Extracts eBay XML data (site ID, item numbers)

- Retrieves customer name, address, phone, email, and buyer ID

- Fetches shipping carrier, service, and tracking numbers

- Retrieves item details (channel SKU, internal SKU, product title, price, quantity, barcode)

- Gets warehouse bin or rack location

👉 Supports audits, reporting, dispute resolution, and operational visibility.

## When It Runs

Used for eBay order reporting and operational analysis.

**Typically runs:**

- Manual execution for reporting

- During customer or shipping investigations

- For order audits and dispute handling

## Parameters

<ParamField path="startDate" param-type="nvarchar" required="true" deprecated="false">
  Start date for processed orders.\
  Example: '01 Nov 2018'
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="endDate" param-type="nvarchar" required="true" deprecated="false">
  End date for processed orders.\
  Example: '21 Feb 2019'
</ParamField>

<Callout kind="info" collapsed="false">
  The script consolidates order, item, and XML data to generate a full eBay order report.
</Callout>

## Process Flow Step-by-Step

<Steps>
  <Step title="Set Date Range" icon="calendar" title-type="p">
    Define startDate and endDate to filter processed orders.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Filter Orders" icon="filter" title-type="p">
    Include only eBay orders for subsource EBAY1 with status Paid, Processed, or Dispatched.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Get Order Details" icon="list" title-type="p">
    Retrieve order ID, channel order ID, order date, processed date, total, tax, and currency.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Extract eBay XML Data" icon="file-text" title-type="p">
    Convert stored XML and extract site ID and item numbers.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Get Customer Details" icon="user" title-type="p">
    Retrieve customer name, address, city, postcode, country, phone, email, and buyer ID.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Get Shipping Details" icon="truck" title-type="p">
    Retrieve carrier, shipping service, and tracking number.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Get Item Details" icon="package" title-type="p">
    Retrieve channel SKU, internal SKU, product title, price, quantity, and barcode.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Get Warehouse Information" icon="map-pin" title-type="p">
    Retrieve bin or rack location for each item.
  </Step>
</Steps>
