## Overview

The **RIS-SC-2260 – Sales Value By Brand with SKU** report provides a detailed breakdown of sales performance by brand and SKU.

It aggregates order data to show total orders, quantity sold, and total revenue, helping analyze brand-level performance.

<Callout kind="tip" collapsed="false">
  Uses extended properties (brand) to dynamically group products without requiring fixed brand fields.
</Callout>

---

## Purpose

This report:

- Groups sales data by **Brand + SKU**

- Calculates **total orders**

- Calculates **total quantity sold**

- Calculates **total sales value**

- Supports **brand-based filtering**

👉 In short:\
Analyze brand performance with SKU-level insights.

---

## How It Works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Fetch Orders" icon="database" title-type="p">
    Retrieves processed orders within the selected date range.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Join Order Data" icon="layers" title-type="p">
    Joins:

    - Order

    - OrderItem

    - StockItem

    - StockItem\_ExtendedProperties
  </Step>

  <Step title="Extract Brand Information" icon="tag" title-type="p">
    Reads brand from:
    Extended Property → 'brand'
  </Step>

  <Step title="Apply Brand Filter" icon="search" title-type="p">
    Uses case-insensitive matching:
    LIKE '%brandName%'

    👉 Allows partial matching
  </Step>

  <Step title="Calculate Total Orders" icon="hash" title-type="p">
    Counts distinct orders per SKU:
    COUNT(DISTINCT OrderID)
  </Step>

  <Step title="Calculate Quantity Sold" icon="box" title-type="p">
    Total Qty = Sum of item quantities
  </Step>

  <Step title="Calculate Sales Value" icon="dollar-sign" title-type="p">
    Total Sales = Qty × Price per unit
  </Step>

  <Step title="Group Results" icon="list" title-type="p">
    Groups by:

    - Brand

    - SKU

    - Title
  </Step>

  <Step title="Sort Output" icon="arrow-down" title-type="p">
    Orders results by highest sales value.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Important Notes

<Callout kind="warning" collapsed="false">
  - Brand is derived from Extended Properties (must exist)

  - Uses LIKE filter → partial matches allowed

  - Case-insensitive comparison applied

  - Uses NOLOCK → may read uncommitted data

  - Only processed orders are included

  - Incorrect or missing brand data may affect results
</Callout>
