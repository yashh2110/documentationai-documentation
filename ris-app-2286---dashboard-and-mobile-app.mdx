## Overview

This mobile application is designed to help employees manage their daily work activities in one place.

It allows users to track attendance, check schedules, apply for leave, view personal and payroll information, and access important documents.

The app provides a simple and user friendly interface for all these features.

**Important note:**\
This mobile application is focused on providing the user interface and user experience only. All data processing, business logic, and system operations are handled by backend services.

---

## Getting Started

When you open the app for the first time

- You will log in using your provided credentials

- Once logged in, you will see your main dashboard

- All features are available through the side menu

The app keeps you logged in securely so you do not need to log in every time.

---

## Dashboard

The dashboard is the first screen you see after login.

It gives you a quick summary of your day.

### What You Will See

- A greeting message

- A timer showing your working hours

- Your current attendance status

- A button to clock in or clock out

### Why It Is Useful

You can quickly check

- Whether you are clocked in or out

- How long you have been working

- Your daily activity

---

## Attendance Management

This is one of the most important features of the app.

### Clock In

When you start your work day

- Tap the Clock In button

- The app records your start time

- Your location is also captured automatically

### Clock Out

When you finish your work

- Tap the Clock Out button

- The app records your end time

- Your total working hours are calculated

### What Happens Behind the Scenes

- The system saves your time securely

- Your working duration is calculated automatically

- Your attendance record is updated instantly

### What You Will See

- Clock in time

- Clock out time

- Current status

- Break details if applicable

- Location status

---

## Leave Management

This section helps you manage your leaves easily.

### What You Can Do

- Check how many leave days you have

- Apply for leave

- Track your leave requests

### Leave Status

Each leave request will have a status

- Pending means waiting for approval

- Approved means accepted

- Rejected means not approved

### How It Works

- You submit a leave request

- The system sends it for approval

- Once reviewed, the status updates automatically

---

## Employee Schedule

This section shows your work schedule.

### What You Will See

- Your weekly schedule

- Working days and timings

- Assigned manager or location

### Why It Is Helpful

- Helps you know your shifts in advance

- Avoids confusion about working hours

- Keeps your schedule organized

---

## Profile Information

This section shows your personal details.

### Includes

- First name and last name

- Email address

- Phone number

- Address

- Work location

### Important Note

- This information is usually managed by the company

- You may not be able to edit everything

---

## Payroll Information

This section shows your payroll related details.

### What You Can View

- National insurance number

- Employment declaration

- Student loan information

- Bank account details

### Important Points

- Most fields are read only

- You cannot change this information directly

- Contact your administrator if something is incorrect

---

## Documents Section

This section allows you to view and sign important documents.

### Policy Documents

- Shows company policies

- You can read and sign them

- If no documents are available, a message is shown

### Contracts

- Shows your employment contracts

- You can review and sign them

- Keeps a record of signed documents

---

## Navigation Menu

The app has a side menu that helps you move between sections.

### Menu Options Include

- Dashboard

- Attendance

- Leave

- Profile

- Payroll

- Documents

- Schedule

- Settings

### How to Use

- Tap the menu icon

- Select the section you want

- The app will open that page

## Location Usage

The app may use your location during attendance.

### Why Location Is Used

- To verify your attendance location

- To ensure correct clock in and clock out

### What Happens

- Location is captured only when needed

- It is stored securely

---

## Background Processing

The app works even when you are not actively using it.

### What It Does Automatically

- Saves your attendance data

- Updates your leave status

- Syncs your schedule

- Keeps your information updated

---

## Common Issues and Solutions

### Clock In Not Working

- Check internet connection

- Make sure location is enabled

### Leave Not Updating

- Wait for approval

- Refresh the app

### Missing Information

- Contact your administrator

### Location Not Detected

- Enable location services

- Allow app permissions

- Session management

## Tips for Best Use

- Always clock in when you start work

- Always clock out when you finish

- Check your schedule regularly
