  Set expiration dates on quotations in Odoo to create urgency, protect pricing,

---
In the Odoo *Sales* application, quotation deadlines can be used to define how long an offer remains valid. Adding an expiration date encourages faster decision-making from customers and helps businesses avoid honoring outdated pricing or terms.

Deadlines are especially useful in competitive or time-sensitive sales scenarios.

## **Quotation expiration**

Odoo allows you to assign an expiration date directly on each quotation.

To set an expiration date:

- Open the **Sales app**

- Select an existing quotation or click **New**

- Locate the **Expiration** field on the form

- Choose a date using the calendar picker

<Image src="https://www.odoo.com/documentation/19.0/_images/quotation-deadlines-expiration-field.png" width="735" height="426" alt="The expiration field on a standard quotation form in Odoo Sales." />

> **Info**
>
> Once the expiration date passes, the quotation is considered no longer valid, helping teams avoid confusion around outdated offers.

> **Tip**
>
> Use shorter deadlines for high-demand products or limited-time offers to create urgency and improve conversion rates.

Customers can clearly see the expiration date when viewing the quotation through the portal.

<Image src="https://www.odoo.com/documentation/19.0/_images/quotation-deadlines-preview.png" width="728" height="352" alt="How customers will see deadlines on Odoo Sales." />

> **Tip**
>
> Always preview quotations before sending to ensure the deadline is clearly visible and aligned with your sales strategy.

## **Quotation template expiration**

Instead of setting deadlines manually each time, expiration rules can also be defined at the template level.

To configure this:

- Go to **Sales app ‣ Configuration ‣ Quotation Templates**

- Open an existing template or create a new one

- Enter a value in the **Quotation expires after** field

This value represents the number of days the quotation remains valid from its creation date.

<Image src="https://www.odoo.com/documentation/19.0/_images/quotation-deadlines-expires-after.png" width="584" height="542" alt="The quotation expires after field on a quotation template form in Odoo Sales." />

> **Info**
>
> When a template is applied, Odoo automatically calculates the expiration date based on this setting.

> **Note**
>
> The automatically generated expiration date can still be adjusted manually on the quotation before sending it to the customer.

> **Tip**
>
> Use template-based deadlines to maintain consistency across your sales team and reduce manual errors.
