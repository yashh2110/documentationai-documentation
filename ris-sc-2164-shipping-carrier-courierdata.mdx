## Overview

The **RIS-SC-2164 – Shipping Carrier CourierData** script creates detailed shipment reports by courier, enabling performance tracking and financial reconciliation.

<Callout kind="tip" collapsed="false">
  In simple terms:\
  It generates a detailed shipment and courier performance report with cost, weight, and item metrics.
</Callout>

## Purpose

This script:

- Fetches shipped orders filtered by courier

- Calculates goods value, postage, total charge, shipment weight, and item count

- Groups shipments by consignment and courier

- Generates a structured report suitable for analytics, finance, and BI dashboards

---

## When It Runs

Typically executed:

- Manually by users or analysts

- Weekly or monthly for courier reporting

- After order processing completion

- For finance reconciliation and shipping performance tracking

---

## Parameters

<ParamField path="@Carrier" param-type="string" required="false" deprecated="false">
  Courier/vendor name filter. Supports partial matching using LIKE '%Carrier%'. Filters recent orders (last 3 weeks).
</ParamField>

<Callout kind="info" collapsed="false">
  Using the carrier filter allows generating reports for specific couriers or all couriers if left blank.
</Callout>

---

---

## Simple Explanation

The script:

1. Filters orders shipped in the last 3 weeks and optionally by courier

2. Combines order, item, and packaging data

3. Retrieves package-level tracking details

4. Calculates goods value, postage, total charge, shipment weight, and item count

5. Groups shipments by consignment and courier

6. Generates a structured report ready for analysis or reconciliation

---

## Step-by-Step Process

<Steps>
  <Step title="Filter Orders" icon="filter" title-type="p">
    Select orders shipped in the last 3 weeks and apply courier filter if specified.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Join Tables" icon="database" title-type="p">
    Combine Order, OrderItem, and OrderPackaging tables, and include shipping package details.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Identify Consignment Number" icon="tag" title-type="p">
    Retrieve tracking numbers from shipping packages, fallback to order tracking if necessary.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Calculate Metrics" icon="calculator" title-type="p">
    Compute shipment details:

    - Goods Value from OrderItem cost

    - Postage from Order

    - Total Charge from Order

    - Weight from OrderPackaging

    - Item Count from OrderItem quantity
  </Step>

  <Step title="Group Data" icon="layers" title-type="p">
    Aggregate shipments by consignment, courier, and order details.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Sort Results" icon="sort-asc" title-type="p">
    Order the output by consignment number for readability.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Execute Query" icon="file-text" title-type="p">
    Generate the final courier-wise shipment report.
  </Step>
</Steps>
