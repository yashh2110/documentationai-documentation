## Overview

The **RIS-RM-1019 – PurchaseOrderCreation** macro automates stock replenishment by identifying items with insufficient inventory and generating purchase orders.

It groups required items by supplier and creates separate purchase orders, ensuring efficient procurement and stock management.

<Callout kind="tip" collapsed="false">
  Ensure supplier details and item configurations are properly set up before running this macro.
</Callout>

## Purpose

This macro:

- Checks stock availability for order items  
- Identifies items requiring replenishment  
- Retrieves supplier details  
- Creates purchase orders grouped by supplier  

👉 In short:  
Automatically generate purchase orders for low-stock items.

## When It Runs

Used for inventory and procurement automation

Typically runs:

- Manually triggered (on selected orders)  
- During replenishment workflows  
- For stock planning and supply management  

## Parameters

<ParamField path="OrderIds" param-type="string" required="true" deprecated="false">
  List of order IDs to process.
</ParamField>

<Callout kind="info" collapsed="false">
  Example:  
  OrderIds = 3001,3002,3003  

  Meaning:  
  Only these specific orders will be checked for stock shortages.
</Callout>

## How It Works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Process Orders" icon="list" title-type="p">
    Retrieve full details for each provided OrderId.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Check Stock Availability" icon="box" title-type="p">
    Compare available stock with ordered quantity.  
    If insufficient → mark for purchase.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Fetch Supplier Details" icon="truck" title-type="p">
    Retrieve default supplier, purchase price, and required quantity.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Group Items by Supplier" icon="layers" title-type="p">
    Group items based on SupplierId to create separate purchase orders.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Create Purchase Orders" icon="file-plus" title-type="p">
    Generate purchase orders with supplier, location, currency (GBP), and dates.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Handle Bundles" icon="package" title-type="p">
    Split composite/bundle items into individual components if required.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Add Items to PO" icon="plus-circle" title-type="p">
    Add items with quantity, cost, and pack size.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Submit Purchase Orders" icon="send" title-type="p">
    Send all items in bulk to the respective purchase orders.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Important Notes

<Callout kind="warning" collapsed="false">
  - Uses default stock location  
  - Uses default supplier only  
  - Creates separate purchase orders per supplier  
  - Bundle items are split into components  
  - Does not merge duplicate items across orders  
  - Currency is fixed to GBP  
  - No validation if supplier is missing  
  - Does not check for existing open purchase orders  
</Callout>
