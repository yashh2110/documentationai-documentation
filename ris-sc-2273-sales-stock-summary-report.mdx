## Overview

The **Sales & Stock Summary Report** combines order data with inventory data to give a complete picture of SKU performance.

It shows how much was sold, how much it cost, and what stock remains—making it ideal for operational and financial analysis.

<Callout kind="tip" collapsed="false">
  This report merges sales and inventory data, helping you track both performance and stock position in one place.
</Callout>

---

## Purpose

This report:

- Tracks SKU-level sales performance  
- Calculates revenue and cost of goods sold  
- Displays current stock levels and value  
- Provides category and source-level insights  

👉 In short:  
See sales, cost, and stock together for better decision-making.

---

## When It Runs

Executed on-demand (SQL report)  

Typically used:

- Daily/weekly sales analysis  
- Inventory planning  
- Profitability tracking  
- Stock monitoring  
- Category performance analysis

## How It Works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Filter Paid Orders" icon="filter" title-type="p">
    Selects orders with valid statuses (Paid/Processed) within the date range.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Convert Timezone" icon="clock" title-type="p">
    Converts order date from UTC to GMT for accurate reporting.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Fetch Order Lines" icon="list" title-type="p">
    Retrieves SKU-level order details including quantity, price, and cost.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Exclude Composite Items" icon="slash" title-type="p">
    Filters out composite (bundle) items to avoid duplication.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Map SKU Categories" icon="tag" title-type="p">
    Links each SKU to its product category.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Fetch Latest Stock Data" icon="database" title-type="p">
    Retrieves the latest stock level and stock value from StockChange.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Get Stock Level Details" icon="layers" title-type="p">
    Fetches:
    - Minimum stock level  
    - Quantity in order book  
    - Available stock  
  </Step>

  <Step title="Calculate Sales Metrics" icon="bar-chart" title-type="p">
    Computes:
    - Sold Quantity  
    - Sales Value (Qty × Price)  
    - Stock Value Sold (Qty × Cost)  
  </Step>

  <Step title="Aggregate Results" icon="grid" title-type="p">
    Groups data by date, source, and SKU.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Sort Output" icon="arrow-down" title-type="p">
    Orders results by date, source, and SKU.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Important Notes

<Callout kind="warning" collapsed="false">
  - Only non-composite SKUs are included  

  - Timezone conversion is applied (UTC → GMT)  

  - Stock values are based on latest StockChange record  

  - Postage is taken at order level (may not distribute per SKU)  

  - StockAvailable = Quantity - InOrderBook  

  - Missing stock values fallback to PurchasePrice calculation  

  - Multiple locations are not separated in this report  
</Callout>
