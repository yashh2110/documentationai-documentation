## **Receipt content**

POS receipts typically include the following information:

- Company logo

- Receipt and order reference numbers

- Custom header and footer text

- Cashier and customer name (if a customer is assigned to the order)

- Full order details, including products, discounts, prices, and payment methods

- Optional QR code or link for generating <a href="https://www.odoo.com/documentation/19.0/applications/sales/point_of_sale/use/pos_invoices.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">**invoices**</a>

## **Configure POS receipts**

To configure receipt settings:

1. Go to <a href="https://www.odoo.com/documentation/19.0/applications/sales/point_of_sale/use.html#pos-use-settings" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">**POS Settings**</a>

2. Scroll to the **Bills & Receipts** section

### **Available options**

- **Header & Footer**\
  Enable this option to customize the text displayed at the top and bottom of receipts

- **Automatic Receipt Printing**\
  Automatically print receipts after payment is completed

- **Basic Receipt**\
  Print simplified receipts without product prices

- **Send via Email / SMS / WhatsApp**\
  Receipts can be sent digitally to customers

  **Note**\
  The **WhatsApp Enabled** option requires the **WhatsApp Messaging** module to be installed

## **Reprint a receipt**

To print a receipt again:

1. Open the <a href="https://www.odoo.com/documentation/19.0/applications/sales/point_of_sale/use.html#pos-use-open-register" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">**POS interface**</a>

2. Click **Orders**

3. Use the filter dropdown and select **Paid** instead of *All active orders*

4. Choose the desired order

5. Click **Print Receipt**

**Tip**

Use the search bar to quickly locate orders by:

- Receipt number

- Date

- Customer
