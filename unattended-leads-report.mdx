  Unattended leads are leads with scheduled activities that are due or overdue.
  Tracking these helps sales teams stay proactive and ensures no opportunity is
  missed.

*Unattended leads* refer to leads or opportunities that have at least one scheduled activity that is either due today or already overdue. Whenever an activity is assigned, the system monitors its due date and notifies the responsible user with reminders.

An *unattended leads report* provides a consolidated view of all such records in the pipeline. This allows sales managers to quickly identify which leads require follow-up and immediate action.

Reviewing this report regularly helps teams stay on track, avoid missed follow-ups, and maintain consistent engagement with prospects.

**Important**

For this report to be accurate and useful, your sales team must consistently use the **Activities** feature within the CRM pipeline.

If activities are not being logged or updated, the unattended leads report will not reflect the true status of follow-ups.

## **Create an unattended leads report**

To generate this report, go to:

**CRM app ‣ Reporting ‣ Pipeline**

This opens the **Pipeline Analysis** dashboard.

Click into the **Search…** bar and remove any default filters to start with a clean view.

**Note**

You may keep the **Created on** filter if you want to limit results by a specific timeframe.

Next, click the dropdown icon next to the search bar to access filtering options, then select **Add Custom Filter**.

## **Add custom filters**

To build an accurate unattended leads report, apply the following filtering conditions:

- **Activities due or overdue**: includes only leads with scheduled activities that are due or past due

- **Assigned salesperson**: excludes unassigned leads

- **Sales team (optional)**: limits results to specific teams

### **Filter for due or overdue activities**

In the custom filter window:

1. Select **Activities**

2. Click the **> (arrow)** to access related fields

3. Choose **Due Date**

<Image src="https://www.odoo.com/documentation/19.0/_images/activities-due.png" width="768" height="411" alt="Custom filter pop-up with emphasis on the options for activities and due date." />

Then:

- Set the operator to **\<=**

- Keep or adjust the date (typically today)

This ensures all activities due up to today are included.

### **Exclude unassigned leads**

Add another rule:

1. Select **Salesperson**

2. Set the operator to **is set**

This removes any leads that are not assigned to a team member.

### **Filter by sales team (optional)**

**Note**

Skip this step if you want results across the entire organization.

To filter by team:

1. Add a new rule

2. Select **Sales Team**

3. Set the operator to **is in**

4. Choose one or more teams

<Image src="https://www.odoo.com/documentation/19.0/_images/configured-custom-rules1.png" width="768" height="254" alt="An example of the Custom Filter pop-up window with all of the rules configured." />

## **View results**

At the top of the filter window, ensure the condition is set to match **all** rules.

<Image src="https://www.odoo.com/documentation/19.0/_images/all-custom-filter.png" width="339" height="209" alt="Example of overdue activities notification in the chatter of a lead." />

Click **Add** to apply the filters.

The report will display all leads with activities that are due or overdue. By default, results are shown as a bar chart grouped by *stage*.

### **Adjust grouping**

To organize the data differently:

- Open the filter dropdown

- Under **Group By**, select **Salesperson**

You can also group by **Sales Team** if needed.

### **Switch to list view**

Click the **≣ (list)** icon in the top-right corner to display the report in a detailed list format.

**Tip**

> Use the column toggle option to customize the report view.
>
> Helpful fields include:
>
> - **Activities**: shows the latest scheduled activity
>
> - **Expected Closing**: estimated closing date
>
> - **Probability**: likelihood of winning the deal
