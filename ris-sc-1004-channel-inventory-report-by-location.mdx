## Overview

The **RIS-SC-1004 – Channel Inventory Report by Location** script aggregates inventory data for a selected sales channel and location. It combines product details, pricing, and stock availability into a single comprehensive report.

<Callout kind="tip" collapsed="false">
  In simple terms:\
  Select a channel and location → View stock levels with product details
</Callout>

## Purpose

This script:

- Retrieves channel-specific inventory

- Maps channel SKUs to internal SKUs

- Includes pricing, fulfillment type, and stock levels

- Provides location-specific insights

👉 Supports inventory visibility and replenishment planning.

## When It Runs

Used for inventory monitoring and reporting.

**Typically runs:**

- Manual execution for inventory checks

- Stock reconciliation

- Amazon FBA inventory review

- Replenishment planning

## Parameters

<ParamField path="Subsource" param-type="nvarchar" required="true" deprecated="false">
  Channel account to filter inventory (e.g., Amazon FBA account).
</ParamField>

<Callout kind="info" collapsed="false">
  Version 1: Fixed parameter for channel account selection.
</Callout>

<Callout kind="info" collapsed="false">
  The script pulls channel data, maps it to internal SKUs, and retrieves stock levels for a specific location.
</Callout>

## Process Flow Step-by-Step

<Steps>
  <Step title="Set Parameters" icon="settings" title-type="p">
    Define subsource, source, and fulfillment type.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Get Channel Data" icon="download" title-type="p">
    Retrieve SKU, barcode, product title, and price from channel tables.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Map to Internal SKU" icon="link" title-type="p">
    Use Stock\_ChannelSKU table to map channel SKU to Linnworks SKU.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Get Stock Information" icon="box" title-type="p">
    Fetch available stock from StockLevel table and match with StockLocation for the correct warehouse.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Apply Filters" icon="filter" title-type="p">
    Filter by:

    - Source (e.g., AMAZON)

    - Subsource account

    - Fulfillment type (e.g., FBA)

    - Matching location
  </Step>

  <Step title="Sort Results" icon="sort" title-type="p">
    Sort by available stock in ascending order to quickly identify low-stock items.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Final Result

<Callout kind="success" collapsed="false">
  - Report displaying:

    - Internal SKU

    - Channel SKU

    - Barcode or ASIN

    - Product title

    - Price

    - Fulfillment type

    - Available stock

  - Provides a clear, location-specific view of channel inventory
</Callout>

## Usefulness

This script is highly beneficial for:

- Tracking inventory in channel-specific warehouses

- Supporting replenishment planning

- Improving stock visibility across channels

- Ensuring stock accuracy between systems

- Assisting operational decision making

## Important Notes

<Callout kind="warning" collapsed="false">
  - Depends on correct mapping between channel SKU and internal SKU

  - Requires accurate stock location setup

  - Filters must be set correctly for meaningful results

  - Large datasets may impact performance

  - Primarily used for channel-specific inventory tracking
</Callout>
