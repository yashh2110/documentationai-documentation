## Overview

The **RIS-SC-1016 – SKU Details** script consolidates product information, including size, colour, and quantity, into a single view. It allows users to search for specific SKUs and quickly access their attributes.

<Callout kind="tip" collapsed="false">
  In simple terms:\
  Find product details including size, colour, and pack quantity in one report for faster lookup and verification.
</Callout>

## Purpose

This script:

- Retrieves SKU, title, size, colour, and pack quantity

- Supports search for specific SKUs or patterns

- Consolidates extended property data for easy reporting

## When It Runs

Manually executed by users or analysts.

**Typically used:**

- Responding to customer queries

- Catalog or product verification

- Checking product variations

## Parameters

<ParamField path="search" param-type="string" required="false" deprecated="false">
  SKU search filter supporting partial matching (e.g., contains text or ending patterns)
</ParamField>

<Callout kind="info" collapsed="false">
  Use flexible patterns for better search results (e.g., `%ITEM1%` or `ITEM%`).
</Callout>

<Callout kind="info" collapsed="false">
  The script links base SKU data with extended properties to provide a complete product overview.
</Callout>

## Process Flow Step-by-Step

<Steps>
  <Step title="Set Search Filter" icon="search" title-type="p">
    Apply a search pattern to find relevant SKUs.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Get Base Product Info" icon="box" title-type="p">
    Retrieve SKU and product title from StockItem.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Fetch Size" icon="ruler" title-type="p">
    Extract size value from extended properties.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Fetch Colour" icon="droplet" title-type="p">
    Extract colour value from extended properties.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Fetch Pack Quantity" icon="package" title-type="p">
    Retrieve pack size or quantity from extended properties.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Apply Filters" icon="filter" title-type="p">
    Exclude deleted and archived items. Filter results based on the search input.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Sort Results" icon="sort-ascending" title-type="p">
    Display results ordered by SKU for easy reference.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Final Result

<Callout kind="success" collapsed="false">
  Report contains:

  - Product Title

  - Colour

## Usefulness

- **Product Lookup** – Quickly find product details

- **Customer Support** – Answer queries about size, colour, and quantity

- **Catalog Management** – Verify and maintain product attributes

- **Variation Handling** – Useful for products with multiple options

## Important Notes

<Callout kind="warning" collapsed="false">
  - Relies on properly maintained extended properties

  - Blank results may appear if size, colour, or quantity is missing

  - Search supports flexible patterns for better lookup
</Callout>
