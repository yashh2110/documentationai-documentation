## Overview

The **RIS-SC-2197 – Monthly Sales Report Script** automates the creation of monthly sales reports for selected sales channels, giving insight into product performance, revenue, and order activity.

<Callout kind="tip" collapsed="false">
  Provides clear visibility into product-level sales and revenue for better business decision-making.
</Callout>

## Purpose

This script:

- Collects item-level sales data from specific channels

- Filters orders within a chosen date range

- Calculates total quantity sold and revenue per item

👉 In short:\
Generates a monthly sales report showing how much each product sold and earned.

## When It Runs

Typically used:

- Manually executed by users or analysts

- During monthly sales reporting

- For business performance tracking

- For revenue analysis

- During channel comparison

- For inventory planning

## Simple Explanation

The script takes orders from specific channels, filters them by date, calculates quantity sold and revenue for each item, and produces a structured report.

## Step-by-Step Process

<Steps>
  <Step title="Set Date Range" icon="calendar" title-type="p">
    Define the reporting period using a start and end date.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Filter Orders" icon="filter" title-type="p">
    Select orders only from Magento and BigCommerce within the specified date range.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Join Tables" icon="layers" title-type="p">
    Combine Order, OrderItem, and StockItem data to get complete item-level details.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Exclude Composite Items" icon="x-circle" title-type="p">
    Ignore parent or bundle items to focus only on individual SKUs.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Calculate Quantity Sold" icon="hash" title-type="p">
    Sum the quantity sold for each item.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Calculate Revenue" icon="dollar-sign" title-type="p">
    Multiply unit price by quantity sold to get total revenue per item.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Group Data" icon="grid" title-type="p">
    Group results by item number, order reference, and composite flag.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Execute Query" icon="play-circle" title-type="p">
    Generate the final dataset with calculated values.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Final Result

<Callout kind="success" collapsed="false">
  The script generates a monthly sales report showing for each product:

  - Item number

  - Associated order references

  - Total quantity sold

  - Total revenue earned

  - Whether the item is a composite product\
    The output provides clear insight into product performance and sales trends.
</Callout>
