## Overview

The **RIS-SC-1009 – Active FBA Inventory Analysis** script provides a detailed overview of Amazon FBA products, their Prime eligibility, and pricing information.

<Callout kind="tip" collapsed="false">
  In simple terms:\
  Find FBA products, check Prime eligibility, and display their price.
</Callout>

## Purpose

This script:

- Identifies active FBA listings on Amazon

- Checks assignment to Prime templates

- Displays product price and fulfillment configuration

- Helps ensure FBA listing compliance and health

## When It Runs

Used for Amazon monitoring and FBA listing audits.

**Typically runs:**

- Manual execution for FBA audits

- Prime eligibility checks

- Listing setup validation

## Parameters

<ParamField path="Source" param-type="string" required="true" deprecated="false">
  Amazon channel source.
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="Subsource" param-type="string" required="true" deprecated="false">
  Amazon account name.
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="FBALocationName" param-type="string" required="true" deprecated="false">
  FBA warehouse location name.
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="PrimeTemplate1" param-type="string" required="false" deprecated="false">
  First Prime template.
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="PrimeTemplate2" param-type="string" required="false" deprecated="false">
  Second Prime template.
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="PrimeTemplate3" param-type="string" required="false" deprecated="false">
  Third Prime template.
</ParamField>

**Example:**\
Subsource `(Your Subsource)` with multiple Prime templates.

<Callout kind="info" collapsed="false">
  The script checks Amazon listings to identify FBA items and verifies their Prime template setup.
</Callout>

## Process Flow Step-by-Step

<Steps>
  <Step title="Set Parameters" icon="settings" title-type="p">
    Define source as Amazon and specify subsource account.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Get Base SKU Data" icon="list" title-type="p">
    Retrieve Amazon SKU and ASIN.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Identify FBA Products" icon="tag" title-type="p">
    Check if item is marked as FBA and assign a flag for FBA status.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Check Prime Templates" icon="shield-check" title-type="p">
    Verify if item is assigned to any provided Prime templates and set flags for each template.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Get Product Price" icon="dollar-sign" title-type="p">
    Fetch listing price from item price table. Mark missing price as possibly deleted.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Apply Filters" icon="filter" title-type="p">
    Include only FBA products and ensure at least one Prime template is assigned.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Sort Results" icon="sort-asc" title-type="p">
    Order results by Amazon SKU.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Important Notes

<Callout kind="warning" collapsed="false">
  - Works only for Amazon channel

  - Depends on correct FBA and template configuration

  - Missing price may indicate inactive or deleted listing

  - Requires accurate parameter setup

  - Read-only script with no data changes
</Callout>
