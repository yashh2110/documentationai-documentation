## Overview

Scripts are designed to automate repetitive tasks and streamline operations across multiple platforms.

They help process data, perform actions, and execute workflows automatically, reducing the need for manual intervention.

These scripts can be used to handle various operational activities such as data updates, order processing, validations, and system integrations.

They ensure consistency, improve speed, and simplify complex processes.

## Summary

Scripts enhance operational efficiency by automating routine tasks and reducing manual effort.

They help teams work faster, minimize errors, and maintain consistency across different systems and workflows.
