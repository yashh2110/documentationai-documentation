---
title: Cannot Login to Rishvi
description: Resolve login problems quickly.
---

## Problem

- Login form reloads
- No error displayed

## Causes

- Incorrect credentials
- Browser cookies disabled

## Quick Fix

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check your username and password">
    Reset password if unsure on the login page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enable browser cookies">
    Rishvi uses cookies for sessions. Enable them in browser settings.
  </Step>
</Steps>
