## Getting Started

To use Rishvi:

1. Visit `https://rishvi.com/signup`
2. Register with your email
3. Verify your account via email
4. Log in at `https://rishvi.com`

## Create Documentation

Click "New Documentation" and follow the prompts.

## Need Help?

Contact support if you're stuck or check guides for walkthroughs.
