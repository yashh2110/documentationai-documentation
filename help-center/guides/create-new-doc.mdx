## Quick Guide

<Steps>
  <Step title="Log in to Rishvi">
    Go to `https://rishvi.com` and sign in.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click 'New Documentation'">
    Find the button on your dashboard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in title and description">
    Enter a name and summary for your documentation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click 'Create'">
    Your new doc appears in the list. Start editing immediately!
  </Step>
</Steps>
