## Overview

Macros are designed to simplify and standardize routine actions by allowing users to perform multiple steps with a single trigger.

They help automate predefined workflows, reducing the need for repetitive manual actions across different systems.

Macros can be used to execute a sequence of operations such as updating data, triggering processes, or applying consistent actions in a controlled and efficient way.

They improve consistency and make daily operations faster and easier to manage.

## Summary

Macros improve productivity by enabling users to perform complex or repetitive tasks with minimal effort.

They help ensure consistency, reduce errors, and allow teams to execute workflows quickly and efficiently across different platforms.
