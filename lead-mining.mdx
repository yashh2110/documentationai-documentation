*Lead mining* allows users to automatically generate new leads inside the *CRM* system. By applying specific filters—such as region, company size, or industry—you can ensure that the leads generated match your target audience and business goals.

## **Configuration**

To enable lead mining:

1. Navigate to **CRM app ‣ Configuration ‣ Settings**

2. Activate the **Lead Mining** option

3. Click **Save**

<Image src="https://www.odoo.com/documentation/19.0/_images/activate-lead-mining.png" width="540" height="250" alt="Activate lead mining in Odoo CRM settings." />

## **Generate leads**

Once enabled, a **Generate Leads** button becomes available in multiple locations:

- **CRM app ‣ Sales ‣ My Pipeline**

- **CRM app ‣ Leads**

- **CRM app ‣ Configuration ‣ Lead Mining Requests**

<Image src="https://www.odoo.com/documentation/19.0/_images/generate-leads-button.png" width="609" height="138" alt="The Generate Leads button to use the lead mining feature." />

Click **Generate Leads** to open a configuration window where you can define your targeting criteria.

<Image src="https://www.odoo.com/documentation/19.0/_images/generate-leads-popup.png" width="768" height="470" alt="The pop-up window with the selection criteria in order to generate leads in Odoo." />

### **Target selection**

You can choose the type of leads you want to generate:

- **Companies**: retrieves general company-level information

- **Companies and their Contacts**: includes both company data and individual contact details

**Note**

When selecting **Companies and their Contacts**, additional filters such as **Role** and **Seniority** become available.

### **Filtering options**

To refine your results, use the following filters:

- **Countries**: target specific geographic regions

- **States**: narrow results further within a country

- **Industries**: focus on particular business sectors

- **Company Size**: enable this option to define a range based on employee count

- **Sales Team**: assign generated leads to a specific team

- **Salesperson**: assign leads directly to a user

- **Tags**: automatically apply tags to categorize leads

**Important**

Ensure compliance with data protection regulations (such as GDPR) when collecting and using contact information.

## **View generated leads**

Once leads are generated, they are automatically assigned based on your selected configuration.

To access them:

- Go to **CRM app ‣ Leads**

- Open a record to review detailed information

The activity thread (chatter) may include additional insights such as:

- Company size

- Technologies used

- Timezone

- Contact details

<Image src="https://www.odoo.com/documentation/19.0/_images/generated-lead.png" width="768" height="522" alt="The chatter thread of a newly generated lead." />

**Note**

If the *Leads* feature is disabled, generated records will appear as opportunities instead.

To enable leads, go to **CRM app ‣ Configuration ‣ Settings**, activate **Leads**, and save.

## **Pricing**

Lead mining operates on a credit-based system.

- Each generated lead consumes **one credit**

- Selecting **Companies and their Contacts** may use additional credits per contact

For detailed pricing, refer to the lead generation service.

To purchase credits:

- Go to **CRM app ‣ Configuration ‣ Settings**

- Locate the Lead Mining section

- Click **Buy Credits**

Alternatively, credits can be managed via the **Settings app** under the services section.

<Image src="https://www.odoo.com/documentation/19.0/_images/view-my-services-setting.png" width="712" height="175" alt="Buy credits in the Odoo IAP settings." />

**Note**

Some users may receive complimentary credits for testing purposes, depending on their subscription or environment.
