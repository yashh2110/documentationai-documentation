## Overview

The **RIS-RM-2171 – Shopify POS Location Stock Deduction** macro identifies Shopify POS orders, determines their fulfillment location, and deducts stock from the correct inventory location while preventing duplicate updates.

<Callout kind="tip" collapsed="false">
  Ensures stock is always reduced from the correct store/warehouse location and avoids double deduction.
</Callout>

## Purpose

This macro:

- Detects Shopify POS orders

- Maps order to correct fulfillment location

- Deducts stock from the right inventory location

- Prevents duplicate stock deductions

👉 In short:\
Automatically reduces stock from the correct location for Shopify POS orders.

## When It Runs

Typically runs:

- Automatically (scheduled or manual trigger)

- After order processing

- During inventory reconciliation

- Within Shopify POS automation workflows

## Parameters

<ParamField path="OrderIds" param-type="none" required="false" deprecated="false">

<Callout kind="info" collapsed="false">

## Simple Explanation

The macro finds Shopify POS orders, checks their fulfillment location, deducts stock from that location, and ensures the update is not duplicated.

## Step-by-Step Process

<Steps>
  <Step title="Fetch Stock Locations" icon="warehouse" title-type="p">
    Get a list of all current warehouse and store locations.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Fetch Processed Orders" icon="search" title-type="p">
    Search for and filter orders processed today (limiting the results to 500).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Loop Through Orders" icon="repeat" title-type="p">
    Process each order individually and log order ID and location.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Identify Shopify POS Orders" icon="shopping-cart" title-type="p">
    Check for extended property: "SHOPIFY\_POS". Only process matching orders.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Match Fulfillment Location" icon="map-pin" title-type="p">
    Map order location to the correct stock location.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Process Order Items" icon="layers" title-type="p">
    Skip service or empty SKUs. Handle both composite and simple items.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Check Stock History" icon="history" title-type="p">
    Check the item's stock change history to see if the stock was already updated for this specific order. Skip it if it has already been processed.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Deduct Stock" icon="minus-circle" title-type="p">
    Update the stock levels by applying a negative quantity (deduction) to the specific mapped location for that SKU.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Logging" icon="file-text" title-type="p">
    Track updated SKUs, skipped duplicates, and log any errors.
  </Step>
</Steps>
