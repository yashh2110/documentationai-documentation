## Overview

The **RIS-SC-1023 – Retrieve Linked Channel SKUs** script helps analysts identify SKUs with specific naming patterns, map them across channels, and generate a new SKU format for standardization purposes.

<Callout kind="tip" collapsed="false">
  In simple terms:\
  Find SKUs containing LLC, suggest updated SKUs with LLCPRP, and show channel mappings.
</Callout>

## Purpose

This script:

- Retrieves channel-linked SKUs

- Identifies SKUs containing the pattern `LLC`

- Generates new SKU names by replacing `LLC` with `LLCPRP`

- Provides channel mapping information for each SKU

---

## When It Runs

Used for SKU standardization and channel mapping verification.

**Typically runs:**

- Manually executed by analysts

- During SKU renaming projects

- Before bulk SKU updates

---

## Parameters

<ParamField path="SKU" param-type="string" required="false" deprecated="false">
  Base SKU reference; currently not used in filtering.
</ParamField>

<Callout kind="info" collapsed="false">
  The script uses a fixed pattern (`DB-RBN-LLC`) and does not depend on the SKU parameter.
</Callout>

---

## Simple Explanation

The script finds SKUs with a specific naming pattern, checks where they are used across channels, and creates a new version of the SKU with updated naming.

---

## Step-by-Step Process

<Steps>
  <Step title="Filter Target SKUs" icon="filter" title-type="p">
    Select SKUs starting with `DB-RBN` and containing `LLC`.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Get Channel Mapping" icon="link" title-type="p">
    Retrieve linked channel SKUs, including source and subsource information.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Generate New SKU" icon="edit" title-type="p">
    Replace `LLC` with `LLCPRP` to create the updated SKU format.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Combine Results" icon="layers" title-type="p">
    Display original SKU, linked channel SKU, source, subsource, and new SKU together.
  </Step>
</Steps>

---

## Final Result

<Callout kind="success" collapsed="false">
  The script generates a report containing:

  - Original SKU

  - Linked Channel SKU

  - Source and Subsource

  - New SKU after replacement
</Callout>

---

## Usefulness

- **SKU Standardization** – Helps update naming conventions

- **Impact Visibility** – Shows where SKUs are used before changes

- **Channel Safety** – Prevents breaking mappings during updates

- **Bulk Update Preparation** – Prepares data for migration or renaming
