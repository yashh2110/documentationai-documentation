## Overview

The **RIS-SC-1031 – Top 100 Selling Products Overview** script helps analysts and business teams identify the best-selling products and categories over the last 3 months.

<Callout kind="tip" collapsed="false">
  In simple terms:\
  Shows the top-performing products and categories based on recent sales data.
</Callout>

## Purpose

This script:

- Retrieves sales data for the last 3 months  
- Groups products by category  
- Ranks SKUs based on performance  
- Displays the top 100 selling products  

---

## When It Runs

Used for business analysis, inventory planning, and promotion strategy.

**Typically runs:**

- Manually executed by analysts or business teams  
- During monthly or quarterly reviews  
- For identifying best-selling products  
- While planning promotions or restocking  
- For business performance reporting  

---

## Parameters

<ParamField path="None" param-type="fixed" required="false" deprecated="false">
  No manual parameters; the script uses automatic 3-month date logic.
</ParamField>

<Callout kind="info" collapsed="false">
  The script always considers data from the last 3 months based on the current date.
</Callout>

---

## Simple Explanation

The script analyzes recent sales, groups products by category, ranks SKUs, and shows the top 100 products for business and inventory decisions.

---

## Step-by-Step Process

<Steps>
  <Step title="Filter Last 3 Months Orders" icon="calendar" title-type="p">
    Include only orders processed in the last 3 months.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Exclude Composite Items" icon="x-circle" title-type="p">
    Remove child items to avoid duplication in ranking.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Get Product Category" icon="layers" title-type="p">
    Group products based on category from extended properties.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Join Required Tables" icon="link" title-type="p">
    Combine order, item, product, and channel data for analysis.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Calculate Metrics" icon="bar-chart" title-type="p">
    Compute total units sold, number of orders, and total revenue per SKU.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Sort and Limit Results" icon="sort" title-type="p">
    Sort products by order count and return the top 100 SKUs.
  </Step>
</Steps>

---

## Final Result

<Callout kind="success" collapsed="false">
The script generates a report containing:

- Category  
- SKU  
- Product title  
- Units sold  
- Order count  
- Revenue  

Only the top 100 products based on performance are included.
</Callout>
