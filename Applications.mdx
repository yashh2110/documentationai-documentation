## Overview

This application is designed to simplify and automate operational processes across multiple platforms.

It helps teams convert order data into clear and structured documents that can be used directly for manufacturing and dispatch activities.

The system reduces manual effort by automatically processing selected orders, organizing product information, and preparing print ready documents.

It ensures that all required details such as product configuration, dimensions, and customer information are easily accessible in one place regardless of the platform being used.

## Summary

This solution improves efficiency across production and warehouse operations by providing a simple and reliable way to generate accurate documents from order data.

It supports multiple platforms such as Linnworks, Everi, Jushuitan, and others, helping teams work faster, reduce errors, and maintain better coordination across different systems and workflows.
