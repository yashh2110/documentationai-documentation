---
title: Tracking lot or serial number products with FBA orders
description: >-
  When selling products that require lot or serial number tracking through
  Fulfilled by Amazon (FBA), Amazon does not provide tracking details via its
  API. This limitation can cause synchronization issues in Odoo.
---
When working with products tracked by lots or serial numbers, Amazon’s API does not send the specific tracking information for each sale. As a result, Odoo cannot assign the required tracking numbers, which leads to synchronization errors.

To avoid this issue, configure a <a href="https://www.odoo.com/documentation/19.0/applications/sales/sales/amazon_connector/fba.html#amazon-connector-fba-product-kit" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">**product kit**</a> in Odoo that represents the Amazon product. When an <u>FBA</u> order is imported, Odoo processes the kit instead of the tracked product, preventing sync failures.

When <a href="https://www.odoo.com/documentation/19.0/applications/sales/sales/amazon_connector/manage.html#amazon-connector-fba-follow-deliveries" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">**replenishing Amazon stock**</a>, transfer the tracked product to the `WH/Amazon` location. This ensures proper inventory tracking and records stock movement at the time of transfer rather than at the time of sale.

**Note**

The `WH/Amazon` location is automatically created when the first Amazon account is connected.

With this approach, kit product quantities may remain negative. This is expected behavior, as actual inventory is managed through transfers to the Amazon location.

**Important**

This method does not track the exact lot or serial number used in each sale. Its purpose is to allow order synchronization without errors.

## **Settings**

To implement this workflow, ensure the following apps are installed:

- **Sales app**: Required to <a href="https://www.odoo.com/documentation/19.0/applications/sales/sales/amazon_connector/setup.html#amazon-setup" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">**connect an Amazon account**</a>

- **Inventory app**: Needed for managing stock across <a href="https://www.odoo.com/documentation/19.0/applications/inventory_and_mrp/inventory/warehouses_storage/inventory_management/use_locations.html#use-locations-configuration" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">**warehouse locations**</a>

- **Manufacturing app**: Used to create <a href="https://www.odoo.com/documentation/19.0/applications/inventory_and_mrp/manufacturing/basic_setup/bill_configuration.html#manufacturing-basic-setup-bom-setup" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">**Bills of Materials (BoM)**</a> for kits

## **Create a product kit**

To handle tracked products correctly, create a kit that represents the Amazon listing.

1. Go to **Sales app ‣ Products ‣ Products**

2. Click **New**

3. Create a product configured as a <a href="https://www.odoo.com/documentation/19.0/applications/inventory_and_mrp/manufacturing/advanced_configuration/kit_shipping.html#manufacturing-advanced-configuration-kits" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">**kit**</a>

**Important**

Do **not** enable **Track Inventory** for the kit product.

<Image src="https://www.odoo.com/documentation/19.0/_images/product-kit-form.png" width="768" height="414" alt="Example of a product form configured to be a kit in the Inventory app." />

### **Link the Amazon SKU to the product kit**

Next, map the Amazon product to the kit:

1. Go to **Sales app ‣ Configuration ‣ Offers**

2. Click **New**

3. Select the <a href="https://www.odoo.com/documentation/19.0/applications/sales/sales/amazon_connector/features.html#amazon-supported-marketplaces" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">**Amazon marketplace**</a>

4. Choose the kit product

5. Enter the Amazon SKU

**Note**

This step intentionally removes the direct link between the SKU and the tracked product.\
Since Odoo cannot assign lot/serial numbers during sync, keeping that link would cause repeated failures.

By linking the SKU to the kit instead, the system bypasses the tracking requirement and completes synchronization successfully.

<Image src="https://www.odoo.com/documentation/19.0/_images/link-amazon-sku.png" width="768" height="195" alt="Example of a kit product linked to an Amazon SKU in the Offers page in the Sales app." />

### **Create the BoM for the product kit**

Now define the kit structure:

1. Go to **Sales app ‣ Products ‣ Products**

2. Open the kit product

3. Click the **Bill of Materials** smart button

4. Create a <a href="https://www.odoo.com/documentation/19.0/applications/inventory_and_mrp/manufacturing/advanced_configuration/kit_shipping.html#manufacturing-advanced-configuration-kit-bom" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">**kit BoM**</a>

In the **Components** tab:

- Add the original tracked product (lot/serial tracked)

**Important**

The tracked product must be the **only component** in the BoM.

<Image src="https://www.odoo.com/documentation/19.0/_images/amazon-product-bom-kit.png" width="768" height="382" alt="Example of the kit BoM for the kit product to represent the Amazon listing." />

Once configured, Odoo will process Amazon FBA orders using the kit, allowing smooth synchronization while maintaining internal stock tracking through warehouse transfers.
