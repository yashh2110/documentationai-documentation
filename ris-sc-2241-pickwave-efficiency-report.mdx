## Overview

The **RIS-SC-2241 – Pickwave Efficiency Report&#x20;**&#x70;rovides a detailed breakdown of each picking batch (pickwave), helping identify efficiency, workload distribution, and operational performance.

<Callout kind="tip" collapsed="false">
  Use date filters to focus on specific operational periods for better insights into pickwave performance.
</Callout>

## Purpose

This report:

- Evaluates efficiency per pickwave

- Tracks time taken for each batch

- Measures items and orders processed

- Identifies bottlenecks in picking operations

👉 In short:\
Analyze pickwave → measure time & work → evaluate efficiency.

## When It Runs

Typically executed:

- Daily warehouse performance reviews

- Pickwave efficiency monitoring

- Identifying delays or bottlenecks

- Operations analysis

- Process improvement planning

- Manually or via scheduled reports
