## Overview

The **RIS-SC-1019 – Inventory Sales Analysis by Category** script helps monitor sales performance and maintain optimal stock levels by category.

<Callout kind="tip" collapsed="false">
  In simple terms:\
  It shows which products are selling fast and recommends minimum stock levels to avoid shortages.
</Callout>

## Purpose

This script:

- Analyzes recent sales by product category

- Compares sales with current stock levels

- Suggests a minimum stock level based on demand

- Supports stock planning and inventory optimization

## When It Runs

Manually executed by users or analysts.

**Typically used:**

- During stock replenishment planning

- For fast-moving product analysis

- While reviewing recent sales trends

## Parameters

<ParamField path="None" param-type="string" required="false" deprecated="false">
  No manual input is required. The script automatically considers the last 2 days of processed orders.
</ParamField>

<Callout kind="info" collapsed="false">
  Combines sales data and current stock to provide insights for inventory management.
</Callout>

## Process Flow Step-by-Step

<Steps>
  <Step title="Filter Recent Orders" icon="filter" title-type="p">
    Include only orders processed in the last 2 days.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Group by Category" icon="grid" title-type="p">
    Organize products based on their category.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Calculate Sales Metrics" icon="hash" title-type="p">
    Compute for each SKU: total units sold, number of orders, and total sales value.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Get Current Stock" icon="package" title-type="p">
    Fetch current stock quantity from the default location.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Estimate Minimum Stock" icon="calculator" title-type="p">
    Calculate half of the total sold quantity as suggested minimum stock.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Rank Products" icon="list-number" title-type="p">
    Rank products within each category based on sales performance.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Exclude Unwanted Data" icon="trash" title-type="p">
    Remove composite child items and exclude Amazon FBA orders.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Final Result

<Callout kind="success" collapsed="false">
  Report containing:

  - Category

  - SKU

  - Current Stock Level

  - Suggested Minimum Stock

  - Units Sold

  - Number of Orders

  - Total Sales Value

  - Rank within category
</Callout>

## Usefulness

- **Stock Planning** – Decide how much inventory to maintain

- **Fast Moving Item Detection** – Identify top-selling products

- **Category Performance Analysis** – Understand trends across categories

- **Inventory Optimization** – Prevent overstocking or stockouts

## Important Notes

<Callout kind="warning" collapsed="false">
  - Only considers processed orders in the last 2 days

  - Excludes composite child items and FBA orders

  - Depends on accurate stock and order data

  - Read-only report; no changes are made to inventory
</Callout>

## Example Scenario

<Expandable title="Stock Replenishment Example" default-open="false">
  Recent sales data shows:

  - SKU **ITEM1** sold 20 units

  - SKU **ITEM2** sold 10 units

  Report Result:

  - **ITEM1** → Current Stock 5, Suggested Minimum Stock 10

  - **ITEM2** → Current Stock 15, Suggested Minimum Stock 5

  **Insight:** ITEM1 requires urgent restocking, while ITEM2 has sufficient stock.

<Callout kind="tip" collapsed="false">

</Callout>
