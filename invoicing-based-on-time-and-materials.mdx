---
title: Invoicing based on time and materials
description: >
  Time and materials invoicing is used when project scope is uncertain or likely
  to change. This method allows billing for hours worked, materials used, and
  other project-related costs as they occur.
---
Invoicing based on time and materials is ideal when a project’s scope cannot be precisely estimated or may evolve over time.

Unlike a fixed-price contract, where the customer pays a predetermined total regardless of actual costs, time and materials invoicing allows flexibility to account for labor, purchases, travel, and other expenses.

The Odoo *Sales* app enables billing for hours worked, related expenses (like travel or lodging), and purchased items required to complete an order.

## **App and settings setup**

To track project progress accurately, the Odoo *Project* and *Accounting* apps need to be installed.

To add the *Project* app, go to **Odoo dashboard ‣ Apps**, locate the **Project** block, and click **Activate**. The dashboard refreshes and the app becomes available.

Repeat the process to install the *Accounting* app.

Once installed, open **Accounting**, go to **Configuration ‣ Settings**, scroll to the **Analytics** section, and ensure **Analytic Accounting** is checked.

<Image src="https://www.odoo.com/documentation/19.0/_images/analytic-accounting-setting.png" width="548" height="181" alt="How it looks to activate the Analytic Accounting setting in Odoo Accounting Setting page." />

Click **Save** to apply the changes.

Next, go to **Project app ‣ Configuration ‣ Settings**, and in the **Time Management** section, enable **Timesheets**.

Click **Save** to confirm.

<Image src="https://www.odoo.com/documentation/19.0/_images/timesheets-feature.png" width="365" height="137" alt="What the Timesheets feature looks like on the Odoo Project settings page." />

## **Configuring service products**

With timesheets enabled, you can invoice for time spent, but only for products configured correctly.

**Important:** Only products with **Product Type** set to **Service** can be invoiced for time.

To configure a service product, go to **Sales app ‣ Products ‣ Products**, select or create a product. In the **General Information** tab:

- Set **Product Type** to **Service**.

- Set **Invoicing Policy** to **Based on Timesheets**.

- In **Create on Order**, choose **Project & Task** to automatically generate a project and task when added to a sales order.

<Image src="https://www.odoo.com/documentation/19.0/_images/service-product-general-settings.png" width="354" height="181" alt="The correct settings for Invoicing Policy and Create on Order fields for service product." />

**Note:** Choosing **Task** instead of **Project & Task** allows adding the task to an existing project. The **Project** field appears when Task is selected.

## **Recording time on a sales order**

With a configured service product, open **Sales app ‣ New** to create a quotation. Add a customer and the configured product under **Order Lines**, then click **Confirm**.

Two buttons appear: **Projects** and **Tasks**.

<Image src="https://www.odoo.com/documentation/19.0/_images/projects-tasks-smart-buttons.png" width="384" height="89" alt="How the Projects and Tasks smart buttons look on a Sales Order in Odoo Sales." />

Click **Tasks** to open the task form. Under the **Timesheets** tab, employees can be assigned and record hours.

Click **Add a line**, select **Date**, **Employee**, and optionally add a **Description**. Enter hours worked in **Hours Spent**, which updates **Allocated Time** and the **Hours Spent / Remaining Hours** fields automatically.

<Image src="https://www.odoo.com/documentation/19.0/_images/timesheets-tab-on-task.png" width="707" height="363" alt="How the Timesheets tab appears on a task form in Odoo Sales and Odoo Project." />

Repeat for all employees and hours worked.

## **Invoicing time worked**

Return to the sales order (via the **Sales Order** button or breadcrumbs). Hours recorded on tasks are shown in **Order Lines** and the **Recorded Hours** smart button.

To invoice, click **Create Invoice**, choose **Regular Invoice**, then **Create Draft Invoice**. The draft invoice shows work performed under **Invoice Lines**.

**Tip:** Review the **Analytic Distribution** column to ensure accurate time/material allocation.

<Image src="https://www.odoo.com/documentation/19.0/_images/invoice-lines-time.png" width="757" height="312" alt="Invoice draft showing time spent on sales order in Odoo Sales." />

Click **Confirm** to finalize the invoice.

## **Tracking expenses**

Install the *Expenses* app via **Odoo dashboard ‣ Apps**, then activate it.

To add an expense, open **Expenses**, click **New**, fill in **Description**, **Category**, **Total**, **Employee**, **Paid By**, and link the expense to the sales order via **Customer to Reinvoice** and **Analytic Distribution**.

<Image src="https://www.odoo.com/documentation/19.0/_images/expense-detail-form.png" width="777" height="275" alt="How to properly fill out an expense form that's attached to a sales order in Odoo." />

Attach receipts if needed, then click **Create Report**. Submit for approval, and the manager can approve and post journal entries.

### **Invoicing expenses**

Navigate to the linked sales order and click the **Sales Orders** button from the expense report. The expense now appears in **Order Lines** and can be invoiced:

<Image src="https://www.odoo.com/documentation/19.0/_images/invoice-expense-from-sales-order.png" width="791" height="248" alt="An expense appearing on Order Lines tab of a Sales Order in Odoo Sales application." />

Click **Create Invoice → Regular Invoice → Create Draft Invoice**, review, and **Confirm**.

<Image src="https://www.odoo.com/documentation/19.0/_images/customer-invoice-for-expense.png" width="768" height="448" alt="Sample customer invoice for an expense generated from a sales order in Odoo Sales." />

## **Purchasing for sales orders**

Install the *Purchase* app via **Odoo dashboard ‣ Apps**, then activate it.

Create a purchase order under **Purchase app ‣ New**, add a **Vendor**, and under **Products**, enable **Analytic Distribution** via the extra column options.

<Image src="https://www.odoo.com/documentation/19.0/_images/extra-column-analytic-distribution-option.png" width="234" height="214" alt="How to add analytic distribution column on purchase order form in Odoo Purchase." />

Add products, ensure they are **Can be Expensed**, invoiced on **Delivered quantities**, and **At cost** under **Re-Invoice Expenses**:

<Image src="https://www.odoo.com/documentation/19.0/_images/product-form-settings-invoice-purchase.png" width="420" height="340" alt="Product settings for a purchase order to be invoiced on a sales order in Odoo." />

Assign the correct **Analytic Distribution**:

<Image src="https://www.odoo.com/documentation/19.0/_images/analytic-drop-down-distribution.png" width="457" height="381" alt="How to select the Analytic Distribution department from a purchase order in Odoo." />

Confirm the purchase, receive products, handle serial/lot numbers if needed, then create a vendor bill.

<Image src="https://www.odoo.com/documentation/19.0/_images/vendor-bill-draft.png" width="769" height="414" alt="Vendor bill draft for a purchase order to be invoiced to a customer in Odoo." />

**Invoice the purchase** by navigating to the linked sales order. Purchased items appear in **Order Lines**:

<Image src="https://www.odoo.com/documentation/19.0/_images/purchase-order-on-sales-order.png" width="765" height="391" alt="Purchase order product on sales order to be invoiced to customer via Odoo Sales." />

Click **Create Invoice → Regular Invoice → Create Draft Invoice**, review, and **confirm**.

<Image src="https://www.odoo.com/documentation/19.0/_images/draft-invoice-with-purchase-product.png" width="763" height="392" alt="Customer invoice draft with purchase product attached to sales order in Odoo." />

Finally, **Register Payment** to complete the invoicing.
