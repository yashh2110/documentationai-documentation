*Partner Autocomplete* allows you to automatically enrich your contact database with accurate and up-to-date corporate data. When creating a new contact, simply enter a company name in the **Customer** field (`partner_id`), and select a suggested result to instantly populate company details.

This feature helps you save time and access valuable business information that is otherwise difficult to collect manually.

## Key Benefits

Using Partner Autocomplete, you can automatically retrieve:

- Company name and logo

- Phone number

- Email address

- Tax ID (VAT)

- Full address

- Industry classification (UNSPSC tags)

## Important Notes

- A company must **not already exist** in the *Contacts* module before using autocomplete enrichment.

- The feature is designed for **company-level data only**. Individual contact data cannot be searched.

## GDPR & Data Compliance

When working with company data, ensure compliance with data protection regulations such as GDPR.

Odoo limits access to personal data and only provides publicly available corporate information through this feature.

## Configuration

To enable Partner Autocomplete:

1. Go to **Settings → Contacts**

2. Locate **Partner Autocomplete**

3. Enable the feature by checking the box

4. Click **Save**

<Image src="https://www.odoo.com/documentation/19.0/_images/settings-partner-autocomplete.png" width="777" height="283" alt="Enable Partner Autocomplete in Odoo settings" />

## How It Works

When creating a new contact in any module:

1. Start typing a company name in the **Customer** field

2. A dropdown list of suggested companies will appear

3. Select the correct company

4. Odoo will automatically fill in the available company data

<Image src="https://www.odoo.com/documentation/19.0/_images/odoo-autocomplete.png" width="933" height="540" alt="Partner Autocomplete suggestions in Odoo" />

## Tip

You can also use a **VAT number** instead of a company name to find and enrich a contact more accurately.

## Pricing

Partner Autocomplete is an **In-App Purchase (IAP)** feature that uses a credit system:

- Each data enrichment request consumes **1 credit**

- Credits must be purchased in advance

### How to Buy Credits

1. Go to **Settings → Contacts**

2. Find **Partner Autocomplete**

3. Click **Buy Credits**

Alternatively:

- Go to **Odoo IAP → View My Services**

- Select a credit package

## What Happens When Credits Run Out?

If your database has no remaining credits:

- Only limited data will be available

- Typically includes:

  - Company logo

  - Website link

## Free Credits for Enterprise Users

If you are using Odoo Enterprise with a valid subscription, you receive **free trial credits** to test IAP features.

This applies to:

- Demo databases

- Training environments

- One-app-free databases
