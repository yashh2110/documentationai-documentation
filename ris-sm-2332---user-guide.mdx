## Overview

The Monitor Unfulfilled Marketplace Orders feature helps you automatically track orders that have not been processed within your expected timeframe.
Instead of manually checking orders, the system will notify your team when orders remain unfulfilled (Open) for too long.

## What Are "Open Orders"?

Open Orders are:

- Orders received from marketplaces (Amazon, eBay, etc.)

- Not yet: Processed, Picked/packed, or Dispatched

These orders require action from your operations or warehouse team.

## Purpose

This feature ensures that:

- No orders are forgotten

- Delays are identified early

- Teams can act before it impacts customers

## How It Works

1. Orders are imported into Linnworks

2. The system checks all Open Orders regularly

3. It calculates how long each order has been waiting

4. If an order exceeds the allowed time: An alert is sent to your team

## When Will You Get Alerts?

You will receive alerts when:

- An order remains in Open status

- And it has been waiting longer than your defined time limit (e.g., 24 hours)

## What You Will Receive

### Email Notification

You will receive an email that includes:

- Summary of delayed orders

- Key order details such as:

  - Order Number

  - Sales Channel (Amazon, eBay, etc.)

  - Location

  - Order Date

  - Delay time

  - Customer Name

### CSV File (Attachment)

A downloadable file with full details of all delayed orders for easy tracking and reporting.

## What You Need to Configure

Your system administrator will set:

- Time limit for delays (e.g., 24 hours)

- Which marketplaces to include

- Email recipients for alerts

No daily setup is required from end users.

## What To Do When You Get an Alert

When you receive a notification:

1. Open Linnworks

2. Locate the affected orders

3. Check why they are delayed:

   - Out of stock

   - Warehouse delay

   - System/integration issue

4. Take action:

   - Process the order

   - Allocate stock

   - Escalate if needed

## Common Reasons for Delays

- Stock not available

- Orders not picked by warehouse

- Integration delays from marketplace

- Manual processing backlog

## Who Should Use This

- **Operations Team**: Monitor and prioritise orders

- **Warehouse Team**: Process pending orders

- **Support Team**: Handle customer-impacting delays

## Important Behaviour

- Once an order is alerted, it will not be alerted again

- This prevents duplicate emails and noise

- Only new delayed orders will trigger alerts

## Best Practices

- Set realistic time limits based on your workflow

- Monitor alerts regularly (do not ignore them)

- Act quickly to avoid customer impact

- Keep stock and inventory updated

## Example

If your delay threshold is 24 hours:

- Order A (30 hours old): Alert sent

- Order B (10 hours old): No alert

- Order C (26 hours old): Alert sent

## Key Takeaway

This feature gives you automatic visibility of delayed orders, helping your team:

- Stay proactive

- Avoid missed shipments

- Improve customer satisfaction
