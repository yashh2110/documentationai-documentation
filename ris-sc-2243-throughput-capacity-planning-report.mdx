## Overview

The **RIS-SC-2243 – Throughput & Capacity Planning Report&#x20;**&#x20;provides a comprehensive view of warehouse performance by analyzing throughput, working hours, and peak activity periods.

<Callout kind="tip" collapsed="false">
  Use this report to align staffing levels with peak workload periods for optimal efficiency.
</Callout>

## Purpose

This report:

- Measures overall warehouse throughput

- Calculates items and orders processed per hour

- Identifies peak working hours

- Supports workforce and capacity planning

👉 In short:\
Measure work → calculate speed → identify peaks → optimize capacity.

## When It Runs

Typically executed:

- Daily or weekly operations review

- Workforce and capacity planning

- Peak hour analysis

- Warehouse optimization

- Management reporting

- Manually or via scheduled reports

## Simple Explanation

The report evaluates picking activity, calculates throughput (items/hour), and highlights peak workload times to help optimize warehouse operations.
