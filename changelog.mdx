## Upgrading to the Latest Version

Follow these steps to upgrade your Rishvi instance:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Backup Data" icon="database">
    Export your configurations and data from `https://dashboard.example.com/export`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Update SDK" icon="download">
    Use the CodeGroup example above for your language.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Test Integrations" icon="check-circle">
    Verify Linnworks, Odoo, and other services in staging.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Deploy" icon="rocket">
    Switch to production and monitor logs.
  </Step>
</Steps>
