title: Product variants on quotations and sales orders
description: >
  Learn how to use product variants on quotations and sales orders in Odoo using
  the product configurator and order grid entry for efficient variant selection.
---
Before getting into detail about how to use product variants on quotations and sales orders, it’s recommended to learn about [**Product variants**](https://www.odoo.com/documentation/19.0/applications/sales/sales/products_prices/products/variants.html).

Once familiar with the basics, this guide explains how product variants can be added to quotations and sales orders using the *product configurator* or *order grid entry*.

> **Info**
>
> The setting is labeled differently depending on where it appears:
>
> - **Variant Grid Entry** → in Sales settings
>
> - **Order Grid Entry** → in product forms

## Settings

Odoo uses the **product configurator** by default for handling variants. To enable the grid-based selection, you must activate the **Variant Grid Entry** feature.

Navigate to:

**Sales app ‣ Configuration ‣ Settings**

Then scroll to **Product Catalog** and enable:

- **Variant Grid Entry**

<Image src="https://www.odoo.com/documentation/19.0/_images/order-grid-entry-setting.png" width="728" height="265" alt="The variant grid entry setting in the Odoo Sales application." />

> **Info**
>
> The **Variants** feature must also be enabled; otherwise, product variants cannot be used in quotations or sales orders.

> **Tip**
>
> Use **Variant Grid Entry** when dealing with bulk orders or multiple variant combinations (e.g., sizes, colors). It significantly speeds up order creation.

## Product configuration

Once enabled, both options (*Product Configurator* and *Order Grid Entry*) become available on product forms.

To configure:

1. Go to **Sales app ‣ Products ‣ Products**

2. Open an existing product or click **New**

3. Navigate to the **Attributes & Variants** tab

At the bottom, under **Sales Variant Selection**, choose:

- **Product Configurator**

- **Order Grid Entry**

<Image src="https://www.odoo.com/documentation/19.0/_images/attributes-variants-tab-selection-options.png" width="730" height="427" alt="Sales variant selection options on the attributes and variants tab on product form." />

> **Info**
>
> These options appear **only when at least two variant values exist** (e.g., Size: S, M, L).

> **Example**
>
> A T-shirt product with:
>
> - Sizes: S, M, L
>
> - Colors: Red, Blue
>
> will enable both configurator and grid options since multiple combinations exist.

## Product configurator

The **Product Configurator** appears as a **Configure** pop-up when adding a product with variants to a quotation or sales order.

<Image src="https://www.odoo.com/documentation/19.0/_images/product-configurator-window.png" width="732" height="445" alt="The product configurator pop-up window." />

This option allows selecting **one variant at a time**, using a user-friendly interface similar to online shopping.

> **Info**
>
> This is the default behavior if **Order Grid Entry** is not enabled.

> **Tip**
>
> Use the configurator for:
>
> - Custom or made-to-order products
>
> - When users need guided selection
>
> - Low-volume or complex configurations

## Order grid entry

The **Order Grid Entry** appears as a **Choose Product Variants** pop-up when adding a product with variants.

<Image src="https://www.odoo.com/documentation/19.0/_images/choose-product-variants-popup.png" width="732" height="352" alt="The choose product variants pop-up window." />

This interface displays variants in a **grid/table format**, allowing users to:

- Enter quantities for multiple variants at once

- Add all selected variants in a single action

After selecting quantities, click **Confirm** to add them to the quotation.

<Image src="https://www.odoo.com/documentation/19.0/_images/order-grid-entry-order-lines-tab.png" width="710" height="494" alt="Order lines populated after grid selection." />

> **Tip**
>
> Order Grid Entry is ideal for:
>
> - Wholesale or bulk orders
>
> - Fashion/apparel businesses (sizes, colors)
>
> - Reducing manual entry time

> **Example**
>
> Instead of adding:
>
> - Red (S) → qty 5
>
> - Red (M) → qty 3
>
> - Blue (L) → qty 2
>
> individually, the grid allows entering all quantities in one screen and adding them instantly.

> **Info**
>
> This method improves efficiency and reduces errors when working with large variant combinations.
