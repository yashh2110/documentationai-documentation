The *Membership / Partnership* module allows businesses to create and manage memberships while offering them through both sales orders and subscription-based products. This makes it easier to treat customers as members and build structured relationships around them.

With this module, businesses can:

- track membership engagement, inactivity, new sign-ups, and churn trends

- monitor renewals and expiration dates

- manage dues and related payments

- communicate with members through targeted emails based on their status

- assign and manage different membership tiers

- organize exclusive events for members

- maintain detailed member lists with contact and profile information

## **Membership / Partnership module vs. the Membership app**

Starting from version 19.0, the standalone **Membership** app has been replaced by the *Membership / Partnership* module.

This updated module includes all the core capabilities of the previous app but offers improved integration with other Odoo applications. It also removes the dependency on the **Accounting** app, making it more flexible for different use cases.

Upgrading to version 19.0 does not impact or remove any existing membership data.

## **App integrations with the Membership / Partnership module**

Once enabled, the module connects seamlessly with several key applications:

- **Sales**: Sell memberships directly through quotations and orders

- **Subscriptions**: Manage recurring memberships and automate renewals

- **eCommerce**: Allow customers to purchase memberships online

In addition, the module supports **pricelists**, enabling businesses to define pricing rules before selling memberships and apply them afterward as needed.

## **Activating the module**

To enable the *Membership / Partnership* module:

1. Go to **CRM app ‣ Configuration ‣ Settings**

2. Locate the **CRM** section

3. Enable the **Membership / Partnership** option

4. Click **Save**

You can also customize the label used for members or affiliates. By default, this is set to `Members`, but it can be adjusted to better match your business terminology.
