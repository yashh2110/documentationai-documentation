## **Supported features**

The **Lazada Connector** offers the following capabilities:

- Automatically imports confirmed Lazada orders (<u>FBM</u>) into Odoo with status **READY\_TO\_SHIP** or **PROCESSED**, including:

  - Product name

  - SKU reference

  - Quantity ordered

- Updates product stock levels from Odoo to Lazada for merchant-fulfilled operations (<u>FBM</u>)

- Syncs the Lazada product catalog into Odoo or allows linking existing Odoo products with Lazada SKUs

The table below highlights the differences between fulfillment methods:

|                            | Fulfilled By Lazada (FBL)                                     | Fulfilled By Merchant (FBM)                                       |
| -------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Orders**                 | Imports completed orders only.                                | Imports all confirmed and pending shipment orders.                |
| **Stock Management**       | Managed by Lazada and tracked in Odoo via a virtual location. | Managed in Odoo Inventory and synchronized with Lazada.           |
| **Delivery Notifications** | Handled entirely by Lazada.                                   | Delivery updates are retrieved from Lazada and reflected in Odoo. |

**Note**

The **Lazada Connector** is primarily designed for synchronizing order and inventory data. Other processes—such as financial reports, disputes, and refunds—must be handled directly in the *Lazada Seller Center*.
