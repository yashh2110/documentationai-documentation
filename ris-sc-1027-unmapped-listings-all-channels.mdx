## Overview

The **RIS-SC-1027 – Unmapped Listings: All Channels** script helps analysts identify listings from various sales channels that are not linked to inventory SKUs.

<Callout kind="tip" collapsed="false">
  In simple terms:\
  Find channel listings that are not connected to stock items.
</Callout>

## Purpose

This script:

- Retrieves listings from multiple channels (Amazon, eBay, Etsy)  
- Identifies SKUs not mapped to inventory  
- Consolidates all unmapped listings into a single report  

---

## When It Runs

Used for channel integration checks, inventory synchronization, and SKU mapping audits.

**Typically runs:**

- Manually executed by analysts  
- During channel integration checks  
- Before inventory synchronization  
- While onboarding new listings  
- During SKU mapping audits

## Simple Explanation

The script collects listings from various channels, compares them with inventory stock items, and identifies SKUs that are not mapped to any stock item.

---

## Step-by-Step Process

<Steps>
  <Step title="Extract Channel Listings" icon="list" title-type="p">
    Collect listings from Amazon, eBay, and Etsy across multiple accounts.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Check Mapping with Stock Items" icon="search" title-type="p">
    Identify SKUs that do not exist in inventory.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Combine All Unmapped SKUs" icon="layers" title-type="p">
    Merge all unmapped listings into one dataset.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Re-check Mapping" icon="check-circle" title-type="p">
    Verify mapping status using mapping and stock tables.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Add Row Number" icon="hash" title-type="p">
    Organize results per subsource for easy tracking.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Select Final Output" icon="layers" title-type="p">
    Display source, subsource, SKU, listing ID, and mapping status.
  </Step>
</Steps>
