## Overview

The **RIS-RM-2137 – Packaging Selection By Location** macro automatically assigns the right packaging for orders depending on the warehouse they are fulfilled from.

<Callout kind="tip" collapsed="false">
  In simple terms:\
  It automatically selects the right packaging for an order based on where it will be shipped from.
</Callout>

## Purpose

This macro:

- Determines the correct packaging group based on warehouse/location

- Recalculates order dimensions and weight

- Updates the order with accurate packaging details

- Ensures compliance with location-specific shipping rules

---

## When It Runs

Typically executed:

- Automatically or manually during order processing

- Before shipping label generation

- After warehouse allocation of orders

- Within WMS/fulfillment workflows

---

## Parameters

<ParamField path="OrderIds" param-type="list[int]" required="true" deprecated="false">
  List of orders to process. Orders must already be assigned to a warehouse/location.
</ParamField>

<Callout kind="info" collapsed="false">
  This parameter controls which orders the macro will process.
</Callout>

---

## Simple Explanation

The macro:

1. Checks the order’s warehouse/location

2. Selects the correct packaging rules

3. Calculates box size and weight

4. Applies updated packaging to the order

This ensures accurate shipping dimensions and weight for every order based on its fulfillment location.

---

## Step-by-Step Process

<Steps>
  <Step title="Fetch Packaging Groups" icon="package" title-type="p">
    Retrieve all available packaging groups registered in the system (e.g., UK, Germany, USA).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Fetch Order Details" icon="shopping-cart" title-type="p">
    Retrieve targeted orders and identify their assigned fulfillment warehouse locations.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Get Location Data" icon="map-pin" title-type="p">
    Cross-reference the order's location with the master list of active warehouse locations.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Map Location to Packaging Group" icon="layers" title-type="p">
    Assign packaging category based on location:

    - UK → UK Packaging

    - Germany → Germany Packaging

    - USA → USA Packaging

    - etc.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Retrieve Packaging Details" icon="box" title-type="p">
    Get packaging group ID, type ID, and base weight for the selected category.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Calculate Packaging" icon="calculator" title-type="p">
    Determine the final estimated weight, height, width, and depth of the packaged order.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Apply Packaging" icon="truck" title-type="p">
    Update the order's packaging type and dimensions with the newly calculated values.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Logging" icon="file-text" title-type="p">
    Track before and after packaging values for auditing and verification.
  </Step>
</Steps>
