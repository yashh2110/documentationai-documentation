## Overview

The **RIS-SC-1032 – Multi-Shipment Tracking Report** script helps analysts and support teams track orders shipped in multiple packages.

<Callout kind="tip" collapsed="false">
  In simple terms:\
  Shows detailed tracking information for each package when an order is split into multiple shipments.
</Callout>

## Purpose

This script:

- Retrieves package-level tracking for orders  
- Identifies multiple shipments per order  
- Includes carrier and service information  

---

## When It Runs

Used for shipment tracking, customer support, and operational audits.

**Typically runs:**

- Manually executed by analysts or support teams  
- During shipment tracking audits  
- For customer support queries  
- While investigating missing tracking numbers  
- For multi-package shipment analysis  

---

## Parameters

<ParamField path="None" param-type="fixed" required="false" deprecated="false">
  No parameters; the script uses existing shipment data.
</ParamField>

<Callout kind="info" collapsed="false">
  The script does not filter records and returns all eligible shipments.
</Callout>

---

## Simple Explanation

The script links shipment packages to orders, retrieves tracking numbers, shows carrier and service details, and highlights multiple shipments per order.

---

## Step-by-Step Process

<Steps>
  <Step title="Link Shipment to Order Items" icon="link" title-type="p">
    Connect shipment data with order items for proper association.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Link to Order" icon="list" title-type="p">
    Retrieve order ID and associated carrier information.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Get Package Tracking" icon="tag" title-type="p">
    Fetch tracking numbers for each shipment package.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Get Carrier Details" icon="map-pin" title-type="p">
    Resolve shipping carrier and service names.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Exclude Internal Shipments" icon="x-circle" title-type="p">
    Remove shipments without valid external carriers to ensure relevance.
  </Step>
</Steps>

---

## Final Result

<Callout kind="success" collapsed="false">
The script generates a report containing:

- Order ID  
- Tracking number(s)  
- Carrier  
- Shipping service  

Multiple tracking numbers per order are shown when applicable.
</Callout>
