  Create reusable quotation templates in Odoo Sales to speed up the quoting
  process and maintain consistency across customer proposals.
---
Quotation templates in *Odoo Sales* allow you to reuse predefined product and service configurations when creating quotations.

By using templates, sales teams can generate consistent and professional quotations quickly, without rebuilding them from scratch each time.

## **Configuration**

To enable quotation templates:

1. Go to **Sales app ‣ Configuration ‣ Settings**

2. Scroll to the **Quotations & Orders** section

3. Enable the **Quotation Templates** option

This reveals an additional **Default Template** field, where you can choose a template to apply automatically to new quotations.

<Image src="https://www.odoo.com/documentation/19.0/_images/quotations-templates-setting.png" width="768" height="263" alt="How to enable quotation templates on Odoo Sales." />

Once enabled, a **Quotation Templates** link becomes available, allowing you to manage templates directly.

Remember to click **Save** to apply your changes.

## **Create quotation templates**

To create a template:

- Click the **Quotation Templates** link from settings, or

- Navigate to **Sales app ‣ Configuration ‣ Quotation Templates**

<Image src="https://www.odoo.com/documentation/19.0/_images/quotation-templates-page.png" width="768" height="157" alt="Quotation templates page in the Odoo Sales application." />

Click **New** to open a blank template form.

<Image src="https://www.odoo.com/documentation/19.0/_images/blank-quotation-form.png" width="768" height="243" alt="Create a new quotation template on Odoo Sales." />

Start by configuring the main fields:

- **Quotation Template**: Name of the template

- **Quotation Validity**: Number of days the quotation remains valid (set `0` for no limit)

- **Confirmation Mail**: Email template sent after order confirmation

**Tip**

You can create a new email template directly from the **Confirmation Mail** field by typing a name and selecting:

- **Create** (quick creation)

- **Create and edit…** (opens full configuration window)

<Image src="https://www.odoo.com/documentation/19.0/_images/create-confirmation-mail-popup.png" width="768" height="493" alt="Create confirmation mail pop-up window from the quotation template form in Odoo Sales." />

Additional configuration options:

- **Company**: Assign the template to a specific company (multi-company setups)

- **Invoicing Journal**: Define the journal used for invoices generated from this template

- **Online Signature**: Require customer signature (if enabled in settings)

- **Online Payment**: Request payment confirmation (optionally set percentage)

Both signature and payment can be required together.

### **Lines tab**

Use the **Lines** tab to define the core content of the quotation.

You can:

- **Add a product**: Select items from your product catalog

- **Add a section**: Organize items under headings

- **Add a note**: Include additional information or explanations

Products default to a quantity of `1`, but this can be adjusted.

Drag and drop items using the handle icon to reorder them.

**Tip**

Event-related products (like registrations or booths) can also be added by searching for relevant product names.

To remove any item (product, section, or note), click the delete icon on the right side of the line.

### **Optional Products tab**

Optional products are used to suggest complementary items, helping increase the overall order value.

For example, offering accessories alongside a main product.

<Image src="https://www.odoo.com/documentation/19.0/_images/optional-products-on-sales-order.png" width="768" height="100" alt="Optional products appearing on a typical sales order with Odoo Sales." />

To configure:

- Click **Add a line**

- Select a product from the list

These products appear as suggestions on quotations and in the customer portal.

**Note**

Optional products are not required when creating templates.

### **Terms & Conditions tab**

Use this tab to define legal or commercial terms that will appear on the quotation.

Enter your content directly into the editor.

**Note**

Adding terms and conditions is optional but recommended for clarity.

## **Use quotation templates**

When creating a new quotation (**Sales app ‣ New**), select a template from the **Quotation Template** field.

The selected template automatically fills in products, structure, and other predefined details.

To preview how the quotation will appear to the customer, click **Preview**.

<Image src="https://www.odoo.com/documentation/19.0/_images/quotations-templates-preview.png" width="768" height="549" alt="Customer preview of a quotation template in Odoo Sales." />

Use the **Back to edit mode** option in the preview banner to return to editing.

Click **Save** once all changes are complete.

## **Mass cancel quotations or sales orders**

To cancel multiple quotations:

1. Go to **Sales app ‣ Orders ‣ Quotations**

2. Switch to list view

3. Select records using checkboxes

**Tip**

Use the header checkbox to select all records at once.

Then:

1. Click **Actions**

2. Select **Cancel quotations**

<Image src="https://www.odoo.com/documentation/19.0/_images/cancel-quotations.png" width="768" height="205" alt="The Cancel quotations option on the Actions drop-down menu in the Odoo Sales application." />

Confirm the action in the pop-up window to complete the process.

**Note**

- Quotations can be canceled at any stage, even after confirmation

- An error may occur if attempting to cancel a subscription that already has an associated invoice
