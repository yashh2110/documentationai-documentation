## Overview

The **RIS-RM-2020 – PackagingSelectionByLocationRulesEngine** macro automates packaging selection and recalculation based on fulfilment location.

It ensures that each order is assigned the correct packaging group and that dimensions and weight are recalculated accurately for shipping.

<Callout kind="tip" collapsed="false">
  Ensure packaging groups and location mappings are correctly configured before executing this macro.
</Callout>

## Purpose

This macro:

- Identifies fulfilment location  
- Assigns appropriate packaging group  
- Recalculates dimensions and weight  
- Ensures accurate packaging output  

👉 In short:  
Automatically assign and recalculate packaging based on location.

## When It Runs

Used for advanced packaging automation

Typically runs:

- Triggered via Rules Engine  
- On selected orders  
- During order processing or packing stage

## How It Works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Fetch Configurations" icon="settings" title-type="p">
    Retrieve packaging groups and stock locations.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Process Orders" icon="list" title-type="p">
    Loop through each OrderId and fetch full order details.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Identify Location" icon="map-pin" title-type="p">
    Determine fulfilment location for each order.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Map Packaging Group" icon="layers" title-type="p">
    Assign packaging group based on predefined location mappings.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Get Packaging Data" icon="box" title-type="p">
    Retrieve packaging calculation data including weight and dimensions.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Build Packaging Structure" icon="package" title-type="p">
    Create packaging request with items, dimensions, weight, and group.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Recalculate Packaging" icon="refresh-cw" title-type="p">
    Apply recalculation logic to update packaging details.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Log Results" icon="file-text" title-type="p">
    Log updated packaging details including dimensions and weight.
  </Step>
</Steps>

</Callout>
