## **Prerequisites**

Before starting the configuration, make sure you have:

- A Lazada Seller account (**Personal Self-Developed** or **Enterprise Self-Developed**)

- A valid email address and phone number for verification

- A business license (required for **Enterprise Self-Developed** accounts)

- A short description of your integration purpose (e.g., “Connecting Odoo ERP with Lazada for order and inventory sync”)

**Note**

The *Lazada Open Platform* account (used for API access) is separate from your *Lazada Seller account*.

## **Create a Lazada Open Platform account**

1. Go to <a href="https://open.lazada.com/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">**Lazada Open Platform**</a> and click **Sign Up**

2. Complete your profile under **Basic Information**

   For **Partner Type**, select:

   - **Personal Self-Developed** (individual users)

   - **Enterprise Self-Developed** (registered businesses)

**Important**

Avoid selecting other partner types, as they are not suitable for Odoo integration.

1. Fill in your **Personal Information**:

- Provide contact details (phone, email, address)

- Complete verification steps (e.g., OTP)

- Upload your business license (if required)

- Add a short description of your integration use case

1. Click **Submit** and wait for approval (typically a few hours to a couple of days)

2. If rejected, review the feedback and resubmit your application

**Tip**

Ensure all details are accurate to avoid delays—common issues include incorrect partner type or missing documents.

## **Create an app on Lazada Open Platform**

To integrate with Odoo, you need an **App Key** and **App Secret**:

1. Log in to the <a href="https://open.lazada.com/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">**Lazada Open Platform Console**</a>

2. Go to **App Console → App Management → Create App**

<Image src="https://www.odoo.com/documentation/19.0/_images/lazada-open-platform-app-console.png" width="3408" height="1432" alt="../../../../_images/lazada-open-platform-app-console.png" />

<Image src="https://www.odoo.com/documentation/19.0/_images/lazada-create-app.png" width="3416" height="1010" alt="../../../../_images/lazada-create-app.png" />

1. Set **App Category** to **Seller In-house APP**

2. Complete the application form:

- Provide your Odoo database URL

- Add tester credentials (username and password)

- For IP settings, select **IP address(es) unavailable** and mention that the app is cloud-hosted

1. Submit the application and wait for approval (usually within 24 hours)

Once approved, note your **App Key** and **App Secret**

## **Connect Lazada Seller account to Odoo**

1. Install the Lazada Connector from the **Apps** menu in Odoo

<Image src="https://www.odoo.com/documentation/19.0/_images/lazada-connector-odoo-app.png" width="371" height="105" alt="../../../../_images/lazada-connector-odoo-app.png" />

1. Enable **Lazada Sync** from:

   **Sales app ‣ Configuration**

<Image src="https://www.odoo.com/documentation/19.0/_images/lazada-odoo-sales-menu.png" width="2424" height="1484" alt="../../../../_images/lazada-odoo-sales-menu.png" />

### **Add a new shop**

1. Go to **Sales ‣ Configuration ‣ Lazada ‣ Shops**

2. Click **Create New Shop**

3. Enter:

- Shop name (e.g., `Lazada Malaysia`)

- **App Key** and **App Secret**

- Marketplace (e.g., <a href="http://Lazada.com.my" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Lazada.com.my</a>)

<Image src="https://www.odoo.com/documentation/19.0/_images/lazada-connect-new-shop-odoo.png" width="1044" height="419" alt="../../../../_images/lazada-connect-new-shop-odoo.png" />

### **Authorize the account**

1. Click **Create Shop & Authorize**

2. You will be redirected to Lazada

3. Log in and grant access to Odoo

<Image src="https://www.odoo.com/documentation/19.0/_images/lazada-authorize-shop.png" width="2850" height="1462" alt="../../../../_images/lazada-authorize-shop.png" />

After authorization, your shops will appear in Odoo:

<Image src="https://www.odoo.com/documentation/19.0/_images/lazada-odoo-shop-list.png" width="3426" height="472" alt="../../../../_images/lazada-odoo-shop-list.png" />

### **Manage marketplaces**

- Marketplaces are automatically added after connection

- You can remove any marketplace to stop synchronization

- Avoid linking the same shop multiple times

**Important**

To prevent duplicate data, ensure each shop is connected only once. If issues occur, try syncing orders manually before reconnecting.

## **Configure the shop before go-live**

### **Warehouse setup**

- Go to **Sales app ‣ Configuration ‣ Settings ‣ Lazada Shops**

- Select your shop and define the **FBM Warehouse**

By default, all shops share the same stock location. To separate inventory by marketplace:

- Create a duplicate shop configuration

- Assign a different warehouse/location

**Tip**

To reinitialize catalog sync, clear the **Last Catalog Synchronization** field before clicking **Sync Catalog**

### **Product catalog synchronization**

The catalog is automatically matched during the first sync, but manual synchronization is recommended in certain cases:

- Use **Sync Catalog** to fetch active products regularly

- For new databases:

  - Export products from Lazada (with SKUs)

  - Import them into Odoo via **Inventory ‣ Products ‣ Import**

  - Map SKUs to **Internal Reference**

- For existing products:

  - Export both Lazada and Odoo product lists

  - Match SKUs with Internal References in a spreadsheet

  - Import updated data into Odoo

**Tip**

Start with a small dataset to verify mappings before syncing the entire catalog.
