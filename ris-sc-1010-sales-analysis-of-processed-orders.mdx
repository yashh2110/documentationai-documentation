## Overview

The **RIS-SC-1010 – Sales Analysis of Processed Orders** script provides insight into product sales and stock levels over the last 3 days.

<Callout kind="tip" collapsed="false">
  In simple terms:\
  Shows which products are selling the most and whether current stock is sufficient.
</Callout>

## Purpose

This script:

- Reviews processed orders for the last 3 days

- Groups products by category (pCategory)

- Calculates units sold, order count, and total sales value

- Compares sales performance with current stock

- Suggests minimum stock levels for replenishment

## When It Runs

Used manually by warehouse managers or analysts.

**Typically runs:**

- Stock replenishment planning

- Fast-moving product identification

- Short-term sales performance analysis

## Parameters

<ParamField path="None" param-type="none" required="false" deprecated="false">
  No manual input required. The script automatically considers the last 3 days of processed orders.
</ParamField>

## Tables Used

- **StockItem** → Product/SKU details

- **OrderItem** → Quantity sold per order

- **Order** → Filters processed orders

- **StockItem\_ExtendedProperties** → Product category (pCategory)

- **ItemLocation** → Bin/rack location

- **StockLevel / StockLocation** → Current stock levels

<Callout kind="info" collapsed="false">
  The script reviews recent sales, groups products by category, and compares sales performance with available stock.
</Callout>

## Process Flow Step-by-Step

<Steps>
  <Step title="Filter Recent Orders" icon="filter" title-type="p">
    Include only orders processed in the last 3 days.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Exclude Unwanted Items" icon="x-circle" title-type="p">
    Remove composite (bundle) items and exclude Amazon FBA items.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Identify Product Category" icon="tag" title-type="p">
    Use pCategory to group items.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Calculate Sales Metrics" icon="dollar-sign" title-type="p">
    For each SKU:

    - Units Sold – Total quantity sold

    - Order Count – Number of orders

    - Total Sales Value – Revenue generated
  </Step>

  <Step title="Estimate Minimum Stock Level" icon="box" title-type="p">
    Calculate Total Sold ÷ 2. Suggest maintaining at least 50% of recent sales as stock.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Fetch Current Stock" icon="layers" title-type="p">
    Retrieve stock from the default location.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Rank Products" icon="award" title-type="p">
    Rank products within each category based on sales performance.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Usefulness

- **Top Seller Identification** – Easily spot best-performing products

- **Stock Planning** – Prevent stock shortages

- **Category Performance Analysis** – Understand demand trends

- **Warehouse Efficiency** – Includes bin location for faster picking

## Important Notes

<Callout kind="warning" collapsed="false">
  - Works on processed orders from the last 3 days

  - Excludes composite and FBA items

  - Depends on accurate product category and stock data

  - Large datasets may impact performance

  - Read-only script with no data modifications
</Callout>
