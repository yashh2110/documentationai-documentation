## Overview

The **RIS-RM-2263 – Remove Company Name from Open Order & Add to Address Line 3** macro ensures address standardization by relocating the company name into Address Line 3 and clearing the Company field.

<Callout kind="tip" collapsed="false">
  Use this macro before shipping label generation to ensure compatibility with couriers that do not support the Company field.
</Callout>

## Purpose

This macro:

- Moves Company Name into Address Line 3

- Clears the Company field

- Applies character length restrictions

- Standardizes address format for courier systems

👉 In short:\
Move company → merge into Address3 → trim → clear field → save.

## When It Runs

Typically executed:

- Before shipping label generation

- For courier system compatibility

- During data standardization

- When courier does not support Company field

- Manually or via automation

## Parameters

<ParamField path="OrderIds" param-type="GUID[]" required="true" deprecated="false">

<Callout kind="info" collapsed="false">

## Simple Explanation

The macro extracts the company name from the order, merges it into Address Line 3, ensures it fits within allowed limits, clears the Company field, and saves the updated address.
