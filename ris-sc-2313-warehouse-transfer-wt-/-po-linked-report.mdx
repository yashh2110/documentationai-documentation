## Overview

The **RIS-SC-2313 – Warehouse Transfer (WT) / PO Linked Report** connects warehouse transfers with purchase orders and stock movement data to provide full visibility of inventory flow.

It enables tracking of stock from **supplier → warehouse → transfer destination** with cost and tax insights.

<Callout kind="tip" collapsed="false">
  This report uses StockChange-based linking logic to connect Purchase Orders with Transfers, even without direct relationships.
</Callout>

---

## Purpose

This report:

- Links Warehouse Transfers (WT) with Purchase Orders (PO)  
- Tracks stock movement across warehouses  
- Provides supplier, cost, and tax details  
- Shows quantity flow and outstanding stock  

👉 In short:  
Track complete inventory movement with financial details.

---

## When It Runs

Executed as an on-demand SQL report  

Typically used:

- Stock reconciliation  
- Warehouse transfer tracking  
- Audit and finance validation  
- Inventory movement analysis

## Data Sources

- warehouse_transfer → Transfer header details  
- warehouse_transfer_item → Transfer line items  
- warehouse_transfer_status → Transfer status  
- StockItem → SKU and product details  
- StockLocation → Warehouse information  
- StockChange → Stock movement tracking  
- Purchase → Purchase order details  
- PurchaseItem → PO tax details  
- ItemSupplier → Supplier SKU mapping  
- Supplier → Supplier information

## How It Works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Aggregate Purchase Data" icon="database" title-type="p">
    Group PurchaseItem records to extract tax values and tax rates.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Fetch Transfer Details" icon="truck" title-type="p">
    Retrieve transfer reference, warehouses, dates, and status.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Map Stock Movements" icon="shuffle" title-type="p">
    Use StockChange table to track stock deductions and match transfer references.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Link Purchase Orders" icon="link" title-type="p">
    Extract PO reference from StockChange source and match with Purchase data using string parsing.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Join Supplier & Item Data" icon="users" title-type="p">
    Fetch supplier name, SKU mapping, barcode, and product details.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Calculate Quantities" icon="calculator" title-type="p">
    Compute:
    - Requested  
    - Sent  
    - Received  
    - Outstanding (Requested - Received)
  </Step>

  <Step title="Calculate Costs & Tax" icon="dollar-sign" title-type="p">
    Compute:
    - Unit Cost  
    - Line Cost  
    - PO Total Cost  
    - Tax values  
  </Step>

  <Step title="Apply Filters" icon="filter" title-type="p">
    Apply dynamic filters for status, source warehouse, and destination warehouse.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Prepare Final Output" icon="file-text" title-type="p">
    Sort and structure data by transfer reference and SKU.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Important Notes

<Callout kind="warning" collapsed="false">
  - PO linking is based on StockChange data (not direct relation)  

  - String parsing is used for PO reference extraction  

  - Data accuracy depends on correct StockChange entries  

  - Large datasets may impact performance  

  - Filters should be used for better efficiency  

  - Outstanding quantity is calculated dynamically  
</Callout>
